# Seat orientation implementation plan

**Goal:** Put seats 1 and 10 at equal-height bottom-left and bottom-right positions without changing seat identities.
**Summary:** Rotate the ten-card arrangement clockwise by half a seat spacing, keeping every card upright.
**Architecture:** Change the shared GameTable angular origin, not game-domain seating/order.
**Tech stack:** React/TypeScript, CSS transforms, Vitest, Playwright.
**Non-goals:** Card-size/radius tuning, game-order changes, Host tile restoration, shader changes.

## Evidence and approach
`apps/web/src/features/table/GameTable.tsx:135-136` calculates angle from FIRST_SEAT_ANGLE and seat.seat. `GameTable.test.tsx:29-40` currently expects 180 + index*36. Computed target origin is 198 degrees (36-degree spacing); seats 1 and 10 then have symmetric horizontal offsets and identical vertical offsets.

1. Add failing shared-table assertions for the new origin and unchanged seat click/keyboard identities; update the obsolete old-angle expectation.
2. Change the shared angular origin only. Retain counterrotation so card content stays upright.
3. Browser-check role giveaway, day/night play and read-only/history tables. Assert seat 1 left of center, seat 10 right, bottom edges aligned within one CSS pixel, and the other eight seats evenly distributed.

**Files:** `apps/web/src/features/table/GameTable.tsx`, `GameTable.test.tsx`; geometry/browser evidence under `.AI/ipad-layout/`.
**Verification:** `npm run test -w @mafia/web -- GameTable.test.tsx`; shared browser matrix from the index.
**Risk:** Special breakpoint offsets can defeat shared symmetry; inspect all seat-anchor rules before declaring success. Clearance is handled by plan 02.
