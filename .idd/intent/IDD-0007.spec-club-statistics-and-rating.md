# IDD-0007.spec-club-statistics-and-rating

## Intent

Let club members compare recorded player performance, inspect the games behind it and use an explainable experimental team-result skill rating. Named tournaments scope statistics and earned rating changes without replacing lifetime ratings or changing gameplay.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 owns club membership, player administration and retained anonymous identities.
- IDD-0002 owns current rules, roles and outcomes; this rating is not official tournament scoring.
- IDD-0003 owns language, native interaction, accessibility and live secret presentation.
- IDD-0004 owns game authority, saving, recovery, correction and deletion.
- IDD-0005 owns supported-data admission and non-destructive compatibility.
- IDD-0006 owns coherent durable storage and small-club operational constraints.

## Behavior

### Access and factual statistics

- Authenticated members can view statistics only for their club. Player-profile administration remains manager-only. Statistics is not a public spectator service and does not expose live roles, private events or consented personal details.
- A compact comparison table shows nickname, games, W/D/L, win rate, team performance, recent form, last played and the separate rating. Search and accessible sorting preserve underlying club ranks. Inclusive UTC dates, team, role and tournament can restrict statistics and history; selected scope and metric-specific observed denominators are visible. There are no edition labels, filters, old-game categories or edition-based exclusions.
- Games played counts known non-cancelled completed human appearances. The separate host and synthetic Casper are not human participants. W/D/L requires a known participant outcome; missing outcome is never loss. Win rate is wins divided by observed W/D/L outcomes, including draws, not by appearances with unknown results. With full facts this is the existing wins/games-played calculation. No relevant observations displays unavailable, not an asserted zero percent ability.
- Player detail provides W/D/L and win rate for each known team and role, fouls and fouls per observed foul-count game, final red-card/elimination counts over known statuses, recorded Best Move result count and hits, and separate known non-cancelled hosting totals. Each metric uses only its actual inputs: missing role, host, date, fouls, status or timeline must not erase another participant's facts or unrelated metrics. Known zero is distinct from unknown. Final status does not imply departure cause or reward survival; nullable Best Move is not an opportunity count, and recorded hits do not establish guess accuracy.
- Recent form uses the latest five known non-cancelled played outcomes with known played times, ordered by that time and stable game identity; undated activity is disclosed, not assigned an invented chronology. Last played is the latest known participant played time, with incomplete-date coverage visible; hosting dates remain distinct. Unknown dates never become update/creation times.
- History exposes saved game identity and available name/time, actual known Casper/roster facts, participant role/team, outcome and applicable reason/adjudication, final fouls/Best Move, tournament and rating explanation. Minimal and partial records remain in unfiltered history; unknown fields are visibly unavailable and cannot satisfy filters that require them. Cancellation remains visible without performance credit. Hosting is distinct from playing. Manager-authorized available timelines remain read-only without replacing the live game or granting non-managers correction; absent timeline detail is a normal unavailable state, not an invalid factual record.
- All retained games can contribute their determinable human factual observations, including Casper games, without an old/current distinction. Retired identities are absent from current leaderboard entries, while their retained participation contributes to surviving peers and historical labels use the localized anonymous fallback.
- Completeness, factual validity and rating eligibility are separate. A completed record remains useful without a full gameplay snapshot, history or derived projection; obtain known facts from its accepted authoritative record, never by replay-based re-adjudication. Disclose missing coverage and use observation-specific denominators rather than all-or-nothing participant/game rejection. Unsupported/corrupt authority and read failures are errors, not permission to silently skip records or display an empty club.
- Club/filter changes clear obsolete rows and suppress late responses. Aggregates cover the complete selected data, not only a history page. Paging of a stable dataset neither duplicates nor loses entries.

### Experimental lifetime skill rating

- The versioned model is `club-team-elo-v1`, an experimental club indicator, not an official Mafia/tennis/padel score, validated individual-skill estimate or cross-club comparison. This calculation identifier is not a per-game rules-engine selector.
- Eligible games have accepted completed results and known played times, ten distinct human participants with valid unique seats, six Citizens, one Sheriff, two Mafia and one Don, consistent teams and known non-Casper complete rosters. A separate host is never rated. Casper games, cancellations and missing/ineligible required rating inputs show an unrated reason rather than an unexplained zero. Unknown roster/Casper information is not proof of eligibility. Edition is not a criterion or unrated reason; no unrelated missing foul, status, Best Move or timeline observation disqualifies otherwise fully determined rating inputs.
- Normal and referee-awarded team wins use the saved outcome. Draws score one half for each side. There are no role, survival, Best Move, check, discipline or hosting modifiers.
- Replay the latest accepted revision of each eligible game by played time ascending, then stable game identity for ties. Initialize each identity at 1,500. Compute each side's arithmetic mean rating before updating any participant. Expected red score is `1 / (1 + 10 ** ((meanBlack - meanRed) / 400))`; expected black is its complement. Every participant receives `32 * (actualScore - expectedScore)` for their side, with simultaneous pre-game expectations.
- Retain calculation precision between games; round only displayed ratings to integers and changes to one decimal. Zero eligible games is unranked, one through nine is provisional, and ten or more is established. The threshold is a presentation convention, not a calibrated confidence interval.
- Established players precede provisional and unranked groups. Established rank is dense by displayed rating; displayed ties share rank, with deterministic nickname/identity ordering only for presentation. Search/filtering does not renumber club rank. Provisional players have no established rank.
- Explanations show before/change/after, pre-game side averages, expectation and saved result. A draw between unequal teams may change ratings. Team means avoid a mechanical head-count advantage but do not prove red/black balance; equal per-player changes across unequal teams are not club-wide zero-sum. These limitations remain visible.
- Lifetime Elo always uses the full eligible club history, independently of visible date/role/team/tournament filters. Corrections, deletion, changed played times and eligibility rebuild affected downstream changes. Profile renaming/retirement does not remove retained identities from the rating history or change peers' ratings.

### Named tournaments

- A tournament is a named club-owned group with a stable identity. A game belongs to zero or one tournament, never implicitly by date or name. Club members who can manage games can create/select tournaments and attach, move or detach games in that club. A trimmed nonempty name is at most 100 characters; case-insensitive duplicate creation selects the existing club tournament instead of making another.
- During player selection, an optional Include in tournament control defaults off for every new game. Enabling it reveals selection or name-only creation. A valid selection is meaningful setup progress; opening the control without choosing is not. Before role assignment, the game's identity and chosen association must be acknowledged rather than silently presented as saved.
- Association survives setup navigation, refresh, retries, play and server resume. It is editable later, including for completed games, without modifying canonical gameplay, results, timestamps or correction rights. Explicit edits are last-commit-wins; ordinary snapshot saves preserve association metadata. Pending work remains visible/recoverable and cannot be silently discarded when switching games.
- Statistics offers All games, No tournament and named events. A selected event shows only matching appearances, results and history; empty or only-unrated events are honest empty/unrated states. Unfinished games never contribute completed performance or reveal live roles.
- Tournament Elo change sums the unrounded full-history per-game deltas for that player's eligible games currently attached to the selected tournament and matching other active filters. Round once at display, show the rated-game denominator, and distinguish no eligible observations from zero net change.
- Compute lifetime history before selecting event entries; interleaved ordinary or other-event games influence the lifetime model but are not included in that event's earned-change sum. Do not subtract ratings across an event's date span or reset all players equally. Event-change sorting is not an official score or champion determination; lifetime Elo and club rank remain separately labelled.
- Moving a game changes event attribution, not lifetime rating. Correcting an earlier result may change downstream per-game deltas in other tournaments; event sums reflect that recalculation. Game/club deletion removes appropriate associations without resurrection or cross-club effects.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Statistics, ratings and tournament metadata preserve accepted gameplay and canonical hashes. Reads must be club-authorized and coherent across their accepted records and associations. Profile resolution is presentation-only. A tournament assignment is not permission to replay or rewrite a stale full gameplay snapshot. No materialized score may become a competing authority over accepted results. Azure backup/recovery guarantees include tournament identities and associations where present.

## Non-Goals

Official tournament points, brackets, rounds, registration, event rename/delete/archive controls, seasons, independent reset-based event ratings, decay, rewards, exports, matchmaking, arbitrary metric builders, behavioural vote/check/shot accuracy, cross-club leaderboards, server-side gameplay adjudication, stronger concurrent-host ownership, or general post-completion editing.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Members see only their club's permitted factual statistics and history; managers retain separate administration and timeline privileges without leaking live secrets.
2. Wins, draws, cancellations, roles, teams, hosting, Casper games, no games and mixed-completeness records produce observation-based totals, denominators and coverage without edition categories or fabricated defaults. Unrelated missing fields never poison all facts.
3. The deterministic eligible-history model handles upsets, draws, simultaneous side updates, ranking ties and the provisional threshold; paging/filtering and storage order cannot retrain it.
4. Repeated saves do not duplicate contributions; corrections/deletion rebuild downstream ratings; retirement preserves peers and removes identifying profile presentation.
5. A host can create/select an event during setup, resume it, finish the game and find its results under that event. Later attach/move/detach survives ordinary saves without altering gameplay.
6. Interleaved event/ordinary games prove event-only delta sums with unchanged lifetime context. Empty/unrated and zero-change cases remain distinct.
7. Authorization, delayed replies, recoverable failures, game/club switching, deletion and supported Azure backup/recovery preserve the same contracts.
8. English/Russian, touch/pointer and narrow/wide layouts expose real table, drilldown, history, event picker and correction interactions without clipped controls or active-game replacement. Native control behavior follows [IDD-0003's Native interaction contract](IDD-0003.spec-host-controls-and-presentation.md#native-interaction).
9. Full facts retain the complete existing Elo algorithm and factual totals except removal of edition-based eligibility exclusion. Partial records may be factually useful but unrated; fully determined rating inputs do not require unrelated observations or optional history.

## Verification

Exercise authenticated result and tournament flows through the application and persisted readback with populated owned fixtures, not only mocked summaries. Combine eligible wins/draws/adjudications with cancelled, Casper, minimal, partial and full records and both available/unavailable timeline detail. Independently omit outcome, played time, host, role/team, status, fouls, Best Move and roster/Casper facts; compare observed totals and denominators, known zero versus unknown, stable undated history and filter behavior. Missing one player's observation must not remove another's known facts. Full inputs preserve Elo values, ranking/rounding and downstream/event deltas apart from formerly edition-excluded games now becoming eligible. Missing unrelated inputs does not block rating; missing actual rating inputs does. Hold requests across changes of club/filter/game and preserve pending work across refresh. Compare canonical bytes before and after metadata edits, retirement and restored backups. Use interleaved event membership and earlier-result corrections to verify full-history rating context and scoped delta accounting. Check visible coverage/explanations, real controls, native field/select/button behavior, permission boundaries and localized accessible layouts.
