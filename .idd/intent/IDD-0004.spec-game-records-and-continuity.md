# IDD-0004.spec-game-records-and-continuity

## Intent

Preserve moderated games across interruptions, make host correction predictable, and distinguish local recovery, server records and explicit deletion.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines who may manage records and how player deletion preserves shared history.
- IDD-0002 defines game outcomes and legal domain behavior.
- IDD-0003 defines auxiliary controls that do not become durable game events.
- IDD-0005 defines compatible storage and guarded clearance.

## Behavior

### Identity, saving and recovery

- The server allocates a game identity before role draws begin. Retrying the same creation request must not create another game.
- A club may have multiple active game records. Authorized hosts can list and resume them without an exclusive claim on a record.
- The browser retains the current snapshot and pending-save retries across refresh. Failed uploads retain pending work rather than reporting it as saved or discarding it.
- Creating a new game requires the server response. Browser-local recovery of an existing game does not guarantee recovery of never-uploaded work after local data is lost.
- Switching or clearing records must not silently discard unresolved saves or overwrite a different record that has replaced the one the user inspected.

### Authority and history

- The browser snapshot is the live-play authority. The server validates authorized club access and transport shape, not domain rules or replay hashes.
- Authorized concurrent saves are last-commit-wins. There are no exclusive host claims or stale-writer fencing guarantees.
- Successful persistence atomically synchronizes the record's related projections.
- Supported game snapshots, canonical events, result projections and generated player-bearing logs contain club-scoped opaque player identity references rather than copied profile names or personal details. Names are resolved separately for display; renaming or retiring a profile does not change canonical game history or its replay hashes.
- Current-format admission may check the structure and club ownership of identity references, including active-game references, without checking domain-rule legality, winners or replay integrity. An authorized current-format save remains last-commit-wins, including after profile retirement when its historical identity references are valid.
- The client validates retained canonical history for Log, Timeline and Preview. Replay integrity does not make server-side rule validation part of the persistence contract.

### Undo, Reset and Finish

- Ordinary Undo is limited to three recent actions and cannot cross the current-day boundary.
- Undo and Timeline Reset retain the chosen history prefix and remove its later portion. They do not create a game event, a preserved alternate branch or a Reset Return operation.
- Any authorized host may use eligible Undo actions after a win, reasoned draw or cancellation until explicit **Finish game**. Detecting or declaring an outcome does not itself finalize correction rights.
- Finish locks client Undo and Reset. It does not require server-side immutability of the closed game; authorized corrections or deletion remain permissible.

### Results and deletion

- Completed history includes red wins, black wins, draws and cancellations, with applicable reasons and host/participant results.
- History can be filtered by inclusive UTC date range, player and outcome. Results must reflect the accepted snapshot, including authorized corrections.
- Deleting a player preserves completed games under IDD-0001 rather than erasing other participants' history.
- A game is removed locally only after the server acknowledges deletion. A stale update or retry of its original creation request must not recreate it.
- Clearing browser storage is not deletion of server-side records, players or results.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Browser authority, club authorization, atomic persistence and non-resurrecting deletion are distinct guarantees. Last-write-wins is not conflict-free multi-host coordination. Local durability must not be represented as server acknowledgment, and client replay validation must not be represented as server validation.

## Non-Goals

- Offline creation without a server-issued identity, realtime exclusive-host coordination or conflict-free cross-device synchronization.
- Server replay validation, stale-writer fencing or immutable completed records.
- Recovery of unsent work after its only local copy is lost.
- An alternate-history tree, Reset Return or a full post-completion editing interface.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Creation retries retain one server-issued game identity; role draws cannot outrun its creation.
2. Multiple active club games can be listed and resumed with correct identities and isolation.
3. Refresh and upload failures preserve pending work; switching or clearing does not silently overwrite unexpected local data.
4. Authorized snapshot saves obey last-write-wins and keep projections synchronized without claiming server domain validation.
5. Undo respects its action/day limits and remains available after wins, draws and cancellations until Finish. Finish locks client correction without imposing API immutability.
6. Log, Timeline and Preview reject invalid retained canonical history; correction truncates rather than branches it.
7. Results and inclusive filters reflect accepted state. Game deletion cannot be undone by a stale save or original creation retry.
8. Supported records and generated logs carry identity references only; a profile rename, directory refresh or permitted retirement leaves their canonical hashes unchanged, while history resolves available names and displays an anonymous fallback for retired identities.

## Verification

Exercise creation retries, failed uploads, refresh recovery, concurrent authorized saves, multi-record switching and unexpected local-record replacement. Verify persisted projections as well as responses. Cover correction limits and the outcome-versus-Finish boundary for all outcomes, canonical history validation, completed-history filters, player-deletion preservation and deletion non-resurrection. Compare canonical hashes and retained per-player outcomes before and after rename and retirement; prove identity references cannot cross clubs or be activated by a concurrent deletion/save race without adding server-side gameplay validation.
