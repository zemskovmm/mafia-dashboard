# IDD-0004.spec-game-records-and-continuity

## Intent

Preserve moderated games across interruptions, make host correction predictable, and distinguish local recovery, server records and explicit deletion.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines who may manage records and how player deletion preserves shared history.
- IDD-0002 defines game outcomes and legal domain behavior.
- IDD-0003 defines auxiliary controls that do not become durable game events.
- IDD-0005 defines compatible storage and guarded clearance.
- IDD-0007 defines statistics, rating and named tournament scope.

## Behavior

### Identity, saving and recovery

- An unfinished game begins with the first meaningful setup change, including host selection, any seat assignment, enabling Casper or choosing a valid tournament. Partial selections are saved; merely opening untouched setup or a tournament picker without choosing does not create a game.
- The server allocates a game identity before role draws begin. Retrying the same creation request must not create another game. Setup edits made during creation or retries remain recoverable and eventually update that same record without being reverted by an earlier response.
- Refresh and authorized server resume restore the setup stage, host, seat order and empty seats, Casper choice, role assignments and remaining draw, game-specific settings and selected playlist. Valid saved player references must not be cleared by incomplete profile loading or another browser's local availability filter. Actual roster changes still invalidate incompatible role progress.
- The captured rules edition survives setup edits, refresh, pending retries, Undo/Reset and server resume. Previously saved club-deviation acknowledgements remain inert historical metadata: preserve them without generating new acknowledgements, requiring consent or displaying warnings. Unsupported editions cannot become editable through a displayed-snapshot fallback after failed admission.
- Pre-planning seat corrections persist atomically on the same game identity: save the accepted seat order together with the cleared deal, never a transient half-swap. Unchanged back navigation and provisional search/drag interactions preserve role progress. Reload or delayed creation/save responses must not restore discarded assignments after a committed correction.
- Clearing, assigning or rerolling a role persists the accepted assignments and available pool together on that same game identity, preserving other seats. Role-bearing setup logs remain secret.
- A club may have multiple active game records. Authorized hosts can list and resume them without an exclusive claim on a record.
- The browser retains the current snapshot and pending-save retries across refresh. Failed uploads retain pending work rather than reporting it as saved or discarding it.
- Creating a new game requires the server response. Browser-local recovery of an existing game does not guarantee recovery of never-uploaded work after local data is lost.
- Switching or clearing records must not silently discard unresolved saves or overwrite a different record that has replaced the one the user inspected.

### Authority and history

- The browser snapshot is the live-play authority. The server validates authorized club access and transport shape, not domain rules or replay hashes.
- Authorized concurrent saves are last-commit-wins. There are no exclusive host claims or stale-writer fencing guarantees.
- Successful persistence atomically synchronizes the record's related projections.
- Supported game snapshots, canonical events, result projections and generated player-bearing logs contain club-scoped opaque player identity references rather than copied profile names or personal details. Names are resolved separately for display; renaming or retiring a profile does not change canonical game history or its replay hashes.
- Current-format admission may check the structure and club ownership of identity references, including active-game references, without checking domain-rule legality, winners or replay integrity. An authorized current-format save remains last-commit-wins, including after profile retirement when its historical identity references are valid.
- The client validates retained canonical history for Log, Timeline and Preview. Replay integrity does not make server-side rule validation part of the persistence contract.
- Accepted federation ballot closures/review rulings, Best Move spoken prefixes/slip corrections/completion reasons, disciplinary timing and outcome bases are retained decisions, not inferred from the latest screen. Provisional entry and timer ticks remain separate from accepted facts. Replay uses the captured edition and must not fabricate past observations or rewrite old hashes.
- Automatically supplied current-stage penalty timing becomes a retained decision only upon host confirmation. Opening a player shortcut creates no event. Replay preserves the confirmed stage, including completed-shooting-result penalties, rather than deriving it from a later screen; player-specific opposing victory retains its offender, rule, reason and required committee agreement.
- Explicit collective hand-count completion is retained separately from provisional or conclusively determined voting, including zero support without invented individual no votes. Accepted physical-shot observations retain the shooter, seated target and repeated occurrence order privately for host Log/Timeline review. Accepting an observation preserves the provisional assignments on which it was based; subsequent correction cannot silently discard the observation. These facts survive local recovery, save/reload and authorized server resume under the same game identity and remain subject to the existing Undo/history-correction limits.

### Nomination and support attribution

- Retain each round's original nominators and unique additional supporters through local recovery, accepted saves and server resume. A current speaker's reversible selection corrects only that speaker's relationship; later unrelated fouls or accepted actions are not discarded to perform the correction.
- Nomination summaries and counts describe surviving selections rather than an on/off activity stream. Necessary canonical correction facts preserve replay integrity but do not inflate statistical activity. Historical records without support observations have none invented; their bytes, hashes and captured edition remain unchanged. Support is distinct from actual ballot votes and does not change result/rating projections.

### Tournament association and recovery

- A game's optional tournament association is club-owned metadata, separate from canonical gameplay and results. Initial association is saved with game creation; retrying creation returns the current association, not a reset to the original request's choice.
- Authorized hosts may attach, move or detach an active or completed game without replacing its gameplay snapshot, played time or results. Explicit association changes are last-commit-wins; ordinary snapshot saves, including clients omitting tournament metadata, preserve the current association.
- Pending association edits are recoverable across refresh and participate in unresolved-work guards before switching, clearing or releasing a browser record. Delayed responses remain bound to the exact club/game and cannot overwrite a newer local selection or a replacement game. Chosen association is acknowledged before role assignment; no pending operation is falsely displayed as saved.
- Refresh and resume restore the saved association without changing seats, roles or edition. New games default to no tournament. Game and club deletion remove their associations; stale association writes and creation retries cannot recreate a deleted game.

### Undo, Reset and Finish

- Ordinary Undo is limited to three recent actions and cannot cross the current-day boundary.
- Undo and Timeline Reset retain the chosen history prefix and remove its later portion. They do not create a game event, a preserved alternate branch or a Reset Return operation.
- Any authorized host may use eligible Undo actions after a win, reasoned draw or cancellation until explicit **Finish game**. Detecting or declaring an outcome does not itself finalize correction rights.
- Finish locks client Undo and Reset. It does not require server-side immutability of the closed game; authorized corrections or deletion remain permissible.

### Results and deletion

- Completed history includes red wins, black wins, draws and cancellations, with applicable reasons and host/participant results.
- A rules draw retains its rule basis. A referee-awarded opposing victory retains its adjudication reason, rule and relevant identity references without fabricated player deaths. Ordinary red/black win statistics remain meaningful; adjudication detail is not overloaded into a field reserved for draw/cancellation reasons.
- History can be filtered by inclusive UTC date range, player and outcome. Results must reflect the accepted snapshot, including authorized corrections.
- Deleting a player preserves completed games under IDD-0001 rather than erasing other participants' history.
- A game is removed locally only after the server acknowledges deletion. A stale update or retry of its original creation request must not recreate it.
- Clearing browser storage is not deletion of server-side records, players or results.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Browser authority, club authorization, atomic persistence and non-resurrecting deletion are distinct guarantees. Last-write-wins is not conflict-free multi-host coordination. Local durability must not be represented as server acknowledgment, and client replay validation must not be represented as server validation.

## Non-Goals

- Offline creation without a server-issued identity, realtime exclusive-host coordination or conflict-free cross-device synchronization.
- Server replay validation, stale-writer fencing or immutable completed records.
- Recovery of unsent work after its only local copy is lost.
- An alternate-history tree, Reset Return or a full post-completion editing interface.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Partial player setup is saved as an unfinished game from its first meaningful edit; untouched setup creates no record. Creation retries and edits during creation retain one server-issued game identity through setup and play; role draws cannot outrun its creation.
2. Multiple active club games can be listed and resumed with correct identities and isolation.
3. Refresh and upload failures preserve pending work, including partial setup and edits made during creation. Server resume restores the complete setup independently of browser-local availability filters; switching or clearing does not silently overwrite unexpected local data. Direct role corrections restore the same assignments/pool on the same identity, and old acknowledgement metadata never revives consent gates or warnings.
4. Authorized snapshot saves obey last-write-wins and keep projections synchronized without claiming server domain validation.
5. Undo respects its action/day limits and remains available after wins, draws and cancellations until Finish. Finish locks client correction without imposing API immutability.
6. Log, Timeline and Preview reject invalid retained canonical history; correction truncates rather than branches it.
7. Results and inclusive filters reflect accepted state. Game deletion cannot be undone by a stale save or original creation retry.
8. Supported records and generated logs carry identity references only; a profile rename, directory refresh or permitted retirement leaves their canonical hashes unchanged, while history resolves available names and displays an anonymous fallback for retired identities.
9. Accepted partial Best Move, technical dispute/final ruling, pending speech/removal/cancellation, explicit collective-count completion, ordered private physical-shot observations and adjudicated results survive refresh and server round-trip under the same edition. Skip preserves an empty declined attempt; early completion preserves accepted numbers. Log/Timeline retains repeated shot details without leaking them into public history.
10. Optional tournament choice survives creation/retry, refresh, play and resume; later metadata-only reassignment preserves exact gameplay and survives ordinary saves. Pending association edits cannot be silently lost on record switching, and deleted games cannot regain associations.
11. Surviving nomination/support attribution remains correct after corrections, speaker/round changes, refresh, duplicate save retries and server resume. Counts exclude toggle activity and original nominators from additional-supporter totals; canonical history remains intact and actual vote/results projections are unchanged.

## Verification

Round-trip a multi-speaker nomination/support sequence with removals/reapplications and intervening fouls through local recovery, both storage providers, replay, preview and eligible Undo/Reset. Compare exact surviving relations and user-facing summaries, not just save acknowledgements; reject forged/duplicate attribution on client canonical admission while preserving the server's existing transport-only validation boundary. Compare older unsupported-field-free records and hashes unchanged and verify that supports do not become ballot votes or rating events.

Choose an event during setup, hold creation and association responses while changing selection, refresh and resume the same game. Attach, move and detach an old game's association without loading it over the live game; compare exact gameplay/results/hash material and then perform an ordinary save. Verify failed association work remains recoverable and deletion prevents stale assignment resurrection.

Open and dismiss a player penalty without changing saved history, then confirm and restore its selected subject, timing, rule and note. Round-trip a completed-shot-result removal and an opposing-team victory, preserving accepted shot observations and unchanged older event hashes. Replaced games or changed phase context must not receive a stale open-form submission.

Verify partial host/seat selection through refresh and server resume with empty browser-local availability preferences. Edit setup while creation is delayed or retrying, reload, and compare the latest persisted setup and single game identity through role assignment and live play. Verify that clearing a saved selection updates the existing unfinished game and that opening untouched setup creates none.

Clear and reassign a role after a full deal, reload and resume the accepted assignments/pool without changing other seats or identity. Round-trip prior acknowledgement-bearing setups without changing their historical metadata or displaying prompts. Preserve private role logs and unchanged canonical gameplay history.

Exercise creation retries, failed uploads, refresh recovery, concurrent authorized saves, multi-record switching and unexpected local-record replacement. Verify persisted projections as well as responses. Cover correction limits and the outcome-versus-Finish boundary for all outcomes, canonical history validation, completed-history filters, player-deletion preservation and deletion non-resurrection. Compare canonical hashes and retained per-player outcomes before and after rename and retirement; prove identity references cannot cross clubs or be activated by a concurrent deletion/save race without adding server-side gameplay validation.

Round-trip both supported editions, including accepted intermediate judge decisions and pending consequences, through local recovery and both supported storage providers. Read back exact accepted state/projections; an acknowledgement alone is insufficient. Verify that unsupported edition admission cannot fall back to editable gameplay and that adjudication survives Finish without losing its reason or changing participant departure status.

Save and restore zero-support collective completion and repeated same/different-target shot observations with provisional assignments. Compare exact accepted state and private Log/Timeline details after replay, local recovery and server readback; verify public projections exclude shot observations, eligible Undo/history correction truncates the accepted facts, and old supported snapshots/events retain unchanged bytes and hashes without injected defaults.
