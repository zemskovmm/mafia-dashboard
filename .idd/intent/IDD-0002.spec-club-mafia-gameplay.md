# IDD-0002.spec-club-mafia-gameplay

## Intent

Make Club Mafia moderation predictable through explicit legal actions, private role information, correct voting and immediate recognition of a decisive outcome.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines club access and human participant identity.
- IDD-0003 defines host interaction, privacy and auxiliary controls.
- IDD-0004 defines retained history, correction and explicit game finalization.
- IDD-0005 defines supported stored-game compatibility.

## Behavior

### Setup and participants

- A game has ten stable seats: six Citizens, one Sheriff, two Mafia and one Don. Citizens and Sheriff are red; Mafia and Don are black.
- The human moderator is separate from the seated players. Roles are drawn privately before Mafia planning, Sheriff wake-up and day one. Setup has no kill or role check.
- Optional Casper mode uses nine human players and a virtual Citizen in seat ten. Casper cannot host or draw a role. A real human named Casper remains an ordinary player; virtual identity is not inferred from a display name.

### Speeches, nominations and discipline

- Day one opens at seat one. Speeches proceed clockwise among active players. Each later day's opener advances clockwise from the previous actual opener, skipping inactive seats.
- A normal speech lasts 60 seconds; a tie speech lasts 30 seconds. Three fouls impose one 10-second nomination-only turn. A foul issued during a speech does not shorten that speech; an unconsumed limited-turn penalty expires when the day rolls over.
- The current speaker may nominate once, including self-nomination. A candidate may not be duplicated in the nomination order.
- Four fouls or a direct red card remove a player without goodbye. A red card during the day suppresses that day's voting; during an ongoing vote or tie sequence it cancels that sequence.
- Otherwise eligible eliminated players receive a goodbye speech unless muted or a winner has already been declared.

### Voting and host correction

- No nominees means no ballot. A lone nominee on day one is not eliminated by voting. A lone nominee on a later day receives all eligible votes.
- Nomination order remains visible. During an open ballot, the host may select any nominee by clicking that candidate's name in the voting order.
- Until **Finish voting**, the host may add, remove or reassign an eligible voter's vote for any selected candidate. A voter has at most one assignment; moving a vote removes its previous assignment.
- Each deliberate selection of the final nominee assigns only eligible voters still unassigned at that moment to that nominee. This applies equally to initial selection and navigation to that nominee. Removing such an assignment leaves that voter unassigned through rendering and restoration; only another deliberate selection of the final nominee may fill the remainder again. Automatic assignments remain correctable like other votes until the ballot is finished.
- Candidate navigation, completing assignments or reaching the final candidate does not itself resolve voting. Normal ballot resolution requires a complete valid assignment of eligible voters and an explicit **Finish voting** action, available independently of which nominee is selected. Disciplinary cancellation remains a separate rule, not normal ballot completion.
- Vote totals are derived from the assignments. Candidates with the highest count advance to the applicable elimination or tie procedure.

### Ties

- Tied highest candidates receive 30-second speeches followed by a clean revote restricted to those candidates.
- A tie that shrinks to a smaller subset repeats the tie procedure for that subset.
- An unchanged repeated tie uses a decision to eliminate all tied candidates, requiring more than half of eligible voters to support it. When the tie includes every active player, skip that eliminate-all ballot instead.
- Apply multiple eliminations as one outcome before evaluating victory.

### Night and private checks

- All active black-team shooters must agree for a kill. Missing or conflicting shots miss. Agreement on an inactive target has no effect; agreement on an active target eliminates that target.
- Shooter assignments and Don/Sheriff check results are private host information.
- The Don checks whether a seated target is Sheriff. The Sheriff checks a seated target's alignment. A deceased player remains a permissible target.
- An eliminated Don or Sheriff may continue checking on later ordinary nights. A role owner red-carded before the night begins is excluded from that night's checks.

### Best Move

- Best Move is enabled by default as a club setting, copied into each game so later club changes do not silently change an existing game.
- When enabled, an eligible human victim of the first regular night chooses three other active seats before goodbye.
- A virtual Casper victim in a newly created game skips Best Move but retains any otherwise permitted goodbye. Supported saved games retain their recorded Casper Best Move eligibility.
- A deciding elimination takes precedence: no check, Best Move or goodbye occurs after it has established victory.

### Outcomes and finalization

- Red wins when no active black players remain. Black wins when active black players equal or outnumber active red players.
- Declare victory immediately after the deciding elimination, including a night kill, before further checks or goodbye.
- Corrected gameplay rules, including open-ballot correction, apply to newly initialized games in the supported current identity-only format. Older name-bearing saved games are rejected under IDD-0005, not converted to the corrected rules or promised continued play.
- Suggest a draw after three complete cycles without an elimination; the suggestion does not end the game automatically. A draw requires host confirmation and a reason. Cancellation also requires a reason.
- An outcome stops normal gameplay but is distinct from **Finish game**. A win, draw or cancellation remains correctable through the host's permitted Undo actions until explicit Finish, as defined by IDD-0004.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

The same domain rules govern restored games and live moderation. Host correction must not create duplicate votes, apply an action to an ineligible participant or silently bypass outcome precedence. Role secrecy and access boundaries are independent of visual presentation.

## Non-Goals

- Arbitrary player counts, additional role sets or remote player-controlled voting.
- Automatic ballot resolution merely from navigation or timer expiry.
- Continuing night checks or farewell procedures after a decisive elimination.
- Free-form incomplete-ballot overrides or prescribed internal event identifiers.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Ordinary and Casper setup produce the correct role distribution and distinguish virtual identity from a human nickname.
2. Day rotation, nomination eligibility, mute duration and red-card consequences follow the rules above.
3. Any nominee can be selected before Finish voting; eligible votes can be added, removed or reassigned without duplicate assignments. The final nominee fills only unassigned eligible votes on deliberate selection; removal persists through reload until that nominee is deliberately selected again.
4. A normal ballot resolves only after explicit Finish voting with a valid complete assignment, regardless of selected nominee; incomplete ballots cannot finish, and each tie branch has a legal outcome.
5. Missing, conflicting and inactive-target shots behave distinctly. Eliminated role owners and deceased check targets follow the private-check rules.
6. A decisive day elimination, red card or night kill in a newly initialized current-format game declares victory without subsequent checks, Best Move or goodbye.
7. Draw suggestions require confirmation; wins, draws and cancellations preserve the separate Undo/Finish boundary.
8. Best Move configuration and supported saved-Casper semantics survive restoration.

## Verification

Cover complete gameplay paths and rejection of illegal actions, including rotating openers, fouls, tie subsets, all-player ties, reassigned ballots, private checks and simultaneous eliminations. Exercise final-nominee selection by initial selection and navigation, removal through rerender/restoration, deliberate reselection, incomplete Finish refusal and completion from an earlier nominee; compare live, restored and replayed supported current-format behavior without duplicate assignments or resolution. Verify immediate victory precedence at every elimination point in newly initialized games and verify that outcome detection does not prematurely disable the correction rights owned by IDD-0004. Keep unsupported name-bearing saves outside successful playback scenarios.
