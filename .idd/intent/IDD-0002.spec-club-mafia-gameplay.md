# IDD-0002.spec-club-mafia-gameplay

## Intent

Make Club Mafia moderation predictable through explicit legal actions, private role information, correct voting and immediate recognition of a decisive outcome.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines club access and human participant identity.
- IDD-0003 defines host interaction, privacy and auxiliary controls.
- IDD-0004 defines retained history, correction and explicit game finalization.
- IDD-0005 defines supported stored-game compatibility.

## Behavior

### Rules edition

- New games default to the [MAFGAME rules dated 1 July 2026](https://mafgame.org/pages/rules). The rules edition is captured when meaningful setup begins and remains fixed through saving, restoration and correction. The following gameplay requirements describe this edition unless expressly identified as legacy behavior.
- Existing supported identity-only club games retain their original rules and recorded meaning. They are not silently converted or relabeled as federation games; IDD-0005 owns this bounded compatibility promise.
- Standard setup uses ten human players with Best Move enabled. Casper, disabling Best Move, discretionary role rerolls and exceptional non-rule ballot overrides are explicit club deviations, visibly acknowledged rather than represented as federation-compliant play. Recording or correcting a physical role deal is distinct from choosing a new deal for convenience.
- Official game night one is Mafia planning; the first discussion is the zero round. The first shooting night is official night two. Completed game nights and actual Sheriff checks are distinct facts; skipped checks do not count as checks.

### Setup and participants

- A game has ten stable seats: six Citizens, one Sheriff, two Mafia and one Don. Citizens and Sheriff are red; Mafia and Don are black.
- The human moderator is separate from the seated players. Roles are drawn privately. Initial night follows 60-second Mafia planning, Mafia sleeping, masked Don identification, masked Sheriff identification, then 30-second free seating before the zero round. Identification is not an instruction to unmask Sheriff, and setup has no kill or role check.
- Optional Casper mode uses nine human players and a virtual Citizen in seat ten. Casper cannot host or draw a role. A real human named Casper remains an ordinary player; virtual identity is not inferred from a display name.
- Before Mafia planning begins, the host may return from role giveaway to player setup. Returning without edits preserves the draw. Any actual host, player or seat change immediately discards the entire deal, including already revealed roles; continuing starts a fresh randomized draw under the same role-composition rules. No confirmation dialog is required.
- Seat correction exchanges the occupants of exactly two seats, without shifting intervening seats. Moving an occupied human seat to an empty seat leaves the source empty. The host is not a seat occupant; enabled Casper remains fixed at seat ten. Cancelled or invalid drops and unchanged selections do not alter the draw.

### Speeches, nominations and discipline

- Day one opens at seat one. Speeches proceed clockwise among active players. Each later day's opener advances clockwise from the previous actual opener, skipping inactive seats.
- A normal speech lasts 60 seconds; a tie speech lasts 30 seconds. Three fouls remove the next ordinary speech entitlement while retaining nomination. The restriction survives night until that turn is consumed. If three or four players remain at that turn, the player receives 30 seconds instead. A foul issued during a speech does not shorten that speech, and a tie speech does not consume the pending ordinary-turn restriction.
- The current speaker may nominate once, including self-nomination. A candidate may not be duplicated in the nomination order.
- Four fouls or a direct disqualification remove a player without goodbye, at the applicable disciplinary boundary. Removal cancels the current or nearest eligible ballot; a removal after the ballot result affects the following day's ballot. Multiple relevant removals do not create multiple consecutive cancelled days. A sanction on a correctly departing vote or shot victim does not create a new departure or cancel an otherwise unaffected ballot.
- Otherwise eligible eliminated players receive their farewell minute unless the game is decided. A pending ordinary-discussion restriction is not a blanket farewell prohibition.

### Night discipline

- The judge records the violation and its occurrence stage; occurrence, effective exclusion, physical departure and public announcement may happen at different boundaries.
- A red player or Sheriff waking during Mafia agreement is immediately removed and announced. During shooting, the judge completes the count before the prescribed removal/announcement; the disqualified shooter's shot does not count. A legitimate shot victim who is also disqualified after counting starts retains both facts, not an invented inactive-target miss.
- An eligible check already underway is completed before the required departure. Announcements follow the rulebook's night sequence without exposing private check details prematurely. Previously disqualified participants cannot act on later nights.
- Departures change population and first-day eligibility exactly once. An announcement alone is not another departure. Human detection of misconduct remains the judge's responsibility.

### Voting and host correction

- No nominees means no ballot. A lone nominee on the zero round is not eliminated by voting. A lone nominee on a later day leaves with all eligible votes as the judge completes discussion, without an unnecessary open ballot.
- Live calls follow nomination order. The judge announces the complete order twice and accepts votes on the candidate number's second mention, until the judge's stop. Physical vote acceptance is exclusively the judge's decision; an advisory timing indicator cannot determine it.
- The host may inspect any nominee and explicitly correct transcription without changing the live call cursor or allowing a player to recast an accepted vote. One eligible voter has at most one assignment. A foul for a late or withdrawn physical hand does not automatically erase an accepted vote.
- When the last candidate is actually called, unassigned eligible votes go to that candidate. Inspection/navigation alone does not invent votes or repeat the live call. Corrections persist through rendering and restoration.
- **Finish voting** is explicit. A fully recorded ballot or a mathematically conclusive partial ballot may finish; a merely leading candidate cannot. Uncast votes remain distinguishable from actual accepted assignments when the result is determined early. The same determined-result principle applies to collective-lift voting.
- A disputed count immediately suspends further calls and resolution. The judge acknowledges review of the external recording and records the final count before play resumes. That ruling is final for ordinary appeals; authorized clerical history correction remains governed by IDD-0004. The app does not certify that external video was watched.
- Legal skips and disciplinary cancellation are distinct from ballot completion. An exceptional non-rule override requires a reason and a nonstandard-game indication, not an unqualified convenience skip.
- The nomination queue remains judge-private working information; the judge does not disclose it in response to a player's request.
- Vote totals are derived from the assignments. Candidates with the highest count advance to the applicable elimination or tie procedure.

### Ties

- Tied highest candidates receive 30-second speeches followed by a clean revote restricted to those candidates.
- A tie that shrinks to a smaller subset repeats the tie procedure for that subset.
- An unchanged repeated tie uses a decision to eliminate all tied candidates, requiring more than half of eligible voters to support it. When the tie includes every active player, skip that eliminate-all ballot instead.
- With nine active players and three repeatedly tied candidates, skip the lift and proceed to night unless official game night three has completed **and** Sheriff has made at least two actual checks. Neither condition alone suffices.
- On the zero round, a repeated five-way tie does not permit a half-table lift; proceed to night. Preserve nomination order and the ordinary shrinking-tie procedure.
- Apply multiple eliminations as one outcome before evaluating victory.

### Night and private checks

- All eligible black-team shooters must agree for a kill. Missing or conflicting shots, or an actual multiple shot by a shooter, miss. A transcription correction is not an actual multiple shot. Agreement on an inactive target has no effect; agreement on an active target eliminates that target, subject to the night-disciplinary timing above.
- Shooter assignments and Don/Sheriff check results are private host information.
- The Don checks whether a seated target is Sheriff. The Sheriff checks a seated target's alignment. A deceased player remains a permissible target.
- Don and Sheriff have one ten-second check window each on ordinary nights. The judge repeats the target seat; Sheriff receives red as thumb up and black as thumb down. A check and a skipped check are distinct.
- A role owner who departed on a previous day/night cannot keep checking. A non-decisively shot same-night owner may complete that night's check before leaving; a decisive outcome or applicable disqualification takes precedence. Inactive check targets remain permissible.

### Best Move

- Best Move is enabled for standard new games and captured at meaningful setup; later club-setting changes do not alter that game. Explicit nonstandard overrides are identified as such.
- The eligible victim of the first shooting night may make Best Move during night, before morning and farewell, with up to 20 seconds to speak. It is unavailable after two or more participants left during the first discussion day, after a miss/no-effect shot, or on later shooting nights.
- Record up to three accepted seat numbers in spoken order. The accepted prefix may contain zero, one or two numbers when the player declines, time ends, or disallowed speech/interaction ends the attempt. Do not require all three. Record the judge-accepted statement without an active-only target restriction; repeated/self numbers remain part of the statement, while descriptive hits count distinct black seats rather than award tournament points.
- Accepted numbers cannot be replaced or added after completion except an explicit correction of an obvious slip. The judge reads back the accepted numbers and acknowledges acceptance before announcing morning. Normal history-correction rights remain distinct.
- **Skip Best Move** is available when no numbers have been accepted. It records that attempt as declined, leaves game/club Best Move preferences unchanged, and continues through the normal morning/farewell path. Once numbers have been accepted, **Finish early** preserves the prefix rather than erasing it. Timer expiry signals a cutoff but never advances the phase automatically.
- A virtual Casper victim in a newly created game skips Best Move but retains any otherwise permitted goodbye. Supported saved games retain their recorded Casper Best Move eligibility.
- A deciding elimination takes precedence: no check, Best Move or goodbye occurs after it has established victory.

### Outcomes and finalization

- Red wins when no active black players remain. Black wins when active black players equal or outnumber active red players.
- Declare victory immediately after the deciding elimination, including a night kill, before further checks or goodbye.
- After three consecutive completed game nights with no participant-count change throughout that period, award a rules draw at completion of the third night. Count initial planning as game night one; any intervening day or night departure resets the no-departure streak. This adopts the literal game-night reading of rule 6.5.6, not a three-missed-shots substitute. Repeated saves or completion attempts cannot count a night twice.
- The judge may explicitly award victory to the opposing side for a rulebook disciplinary loss (ППК), recording the losing side, applicable offender identity, rule and reason, and committee agreement where required. Do not fabricate eliminations to produce the winner. Human assessment of misconduct is not automated.
- Separate discretionary draws and cancellation require a host reason. A rules draw retains its rule basis; an adjudicated win retains its disciplinary basis and reason independently of ordinary team-win statistics.
- An outcome stops normal gameplay but is distinct from **Finish game**. A win, draw or cancellation remains correctable through the host's permitted Undo actions until explicit Finish, as defined by IDD-0004.

### Supported legacy club games

- Previously supported identity-only club games retain their original setup sequence, day numbering and captured settings, including the documented club-setting fallback for supported old setup without a captured Best Move value and the saved-Casper exception.
- Their open ballot permits any nominee selection, vote reassignment and deliberate final-nominee remainder filling; removed remainder assignments remain unassigned until deliberate reselection. Normal resolution still requires explicit Finish with all eligible assignments. They retain their original tie eligibility rather than acquiring federation restrictions retroactively.
- Their third foul grants the original ten-second nomination-only turn and expires at day rollover; pending mute excludes farewell. Previously eliminated check owners remain eligible according to their original rules. Best Move retains the original exact-three-other-active-seats semantics.
- Their three-cycle draw remains a reasoned host-confirmed suggestion, not an automatic federation draw. Their stored canonical state and hashes are preserved without inserted federation defaults.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

The captured rules edition governs both restored games and live moderation. Host correction must not create duplicate votes, apply an action to an ineligible participant or silently bypass outcome precedence. Role secrecy and access boundaries are independent of visual presentation.

## Non-Goals

- Arbitrary player counts, additional role sets or remote player-controlled voting.
- Automatic ballot resolution merely from navigation or timer expiry.
- Continuing night checks or farewell procedures after a decisive elimination.
- Unreasoned incomplete-ballot overrides, tournament scoring/standings/appeal systems, or prescribed internal event identifiers.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. New games capture the federation edition and correct role composition; club deviations are explicit. Opening identification is masked and followed by free seating without initial checks or kills.
2. Rotation, nomination eligibility, pending speech restriction, late-game 30-second exception and discipline follow the applicable edition and survive restoration.
3. Federation calls stay ordered independently of nominee inspection; accepted votes are unique, corrections persist, and disputed voting cannot progress until the final reviewed ruling.
4. Complete or conclusively decided ballots finish explicitly without fabricated votes; both restricted-tie rules and ordinary shrinking/all-active ties have correct outcomes.
5. Shots distinguish actual multiple firing from transcription correction; private checks distinguish current-night victim eligibility from prior departure and skips from actual checks.
6. Best Move remains night, records accepted prefixes of zero through three numbers and offers Skip or prefix-preserving Finish early. First-day departures control eligibility; completion never silently changes the preference or discards accepted numbers.
7. Decisive outcomes prevent further checks, Best Move and farewell; qualifying rules draws and reasoned opposing-team victories retain the separate Undo/Finish boundary.
8. Removal timing, announcements, combined shot/removal and one affected-ballot cancellation survive reload/replay without counting departures twice.
9. Supported legacy games retain original settings, rules and canonical hashes, including saved-Casper behavior; unsupported name-bearing formats remain rejected.

## Verification

Exercise pre-planning correction after partial and complete role giveaway: unchanged return preserves assignments, while swaps, replacement, clearing and host changes discard all assignments and require a fresh draw. Verify fixed seat identities, empty-seat moves, locked Casper and cancelled/no-op corrections.

Save partial setup, change club preferences, resume and start play; the saved edition/settings must prevail. Resume supported legacy setup without a captured setting and verify its documented fallback, without assigning federation defaults to it.

Exercise complete federation journeys covering ordered voting, correction without recasting, disputed-count recovery, conclusive early close and undecided refusal; test both conditions of the nine-player tie exception independently and zero-round half-table prohibition. Verify night identification, actual/skip check counts, prior-departure eligibility, multiple shooting, the three-night draw boundary and intervening departures.

Exercise Best Move decline, accepted partial prefixes, time/interruption cutoffs and explicit slip correction through refresh, replay and morning/farewell. Verify Skip does not change preferences, and Finish early never discards accepted numbers. Cover carried speech restrictions, short-table entitlement and phase-specific removals, including shot/removal intersection and coalesced next-ballot cancellation. Verify reasoned opposing victory without fabricated deaths and all outcomes' correction/Finish boundary. Compare supported legacy histories and hashes unchanged; keep unsupported name-bearing saves outside successful playback scenarios.
