# IDD-0001.spec-club-access-and-player-identity

## Intent

Provide a club-isolated, host-operated dashboard for in-person Mafia games while respecting player consent and preserving shared game history.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0002 defines participants, roles and game rules.
- IDD-0004 defines game-record access, correction and deletion effects.
- IDD-0005 defines preservation when stored data is incompatible.

## Behavior

### Accounts and clubs

- Users authenticate through Google sign-in. An authenticated user may create a club without a fee; club creation requires the creator's personal-data consent and creates a linked manager profile.
- The server authorizes access against club membership. Selecting or supplying a club identifier does not grant access to that club's data.
- Managers and regular club users may host games and list, open, create, save and delete game records in their own club. Club and player-profile administration remain manager privileges.
- Withdrawing consent for the creator's player profile does not silently remove the creator's club-management access.
- Game records and secret host data are not anonymously accessible. An authenticated, redacted event view is not a public spectator service.

### Player identity and participation

- A player has a required nickname, unique within the club without regard to case.
- Without personal-data consent, the player profile retains only the nickname. Storing personal identity details requires consent and requires first name, last name and email.
- Each player has an opaque, permanent identity scoped to the club. Names and consented personal details belong to the erasable player profile, not the identity carried by game records. A retired identity is never assigned to a different player or offered for new human participation.
- A game host is a separate saved human profile and cannot simultaneously occupy a player seat in that game. Virtual Casper participation is governed by IDD-0002, not by a bypass of account or club authorization.

### Deletion and retention

- A player participating in an active game, whether as host or participant, cannot be deleted.
- When deletion is permitted, retire that player's identity and erase the profile nickname, consented personal details and profile-derived user links/access. Retain the opaque identity, completed shared games, anonymous individual participation/results and other players' results. Profile administration and new-game selection exclude retired identities; historical displays resolve current profiles separately and show a localized anonymous fallback for retired or unavailable profiles.
- Identifying profile changes or deletion do not change the meaning or integrity of supported identity-only game histories. Historical preservation after deletion applies to supported identity-only records; incompatible older named records are handled under IDD-0005, not silently anonymized.
- Club deletion is a distinct operation that removes that club's server-side data.
- Clearing browser data does not delete server-side clubs, players, game records or results.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Club isolation and consent enforcement are server-side security boundaries. Client selection, a guessed record identifier or a permitted action in another club must not bypass them. Retained identity references preserve individual game meaning without keeping erased profile fields in supported game material. Free-text moderator reasons remain free text, not an automatically redacted identity field.

## Non-Goals

- Anonymous game-record access, a public spectator service or a player-phone game client.
- Granting regular members club or player-profile administration merely because they may host games.
- Prescribing a database schema or a particular anonymized placeholder representation.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A regular member can manage game records in their club but cannot manage another club's records or perform manager-only administration.
2. Missing authentication, forged club selection and cross-club record identifiers do not expose or mutate protected data.
3. Nickname-only participation, consented personal details and consent withdrawal respect their respective data boundaries.
4. Setup rejects a host who also occupies a player seat.
5. Active hosting or participation blocks player deletion. Permitted deletion erases the profile, retains the same opaque identity and completed outcomes, including anonymous individual and other-player results, without keeping copied names in supported game records.
6. Browser clearing and server-side deletion have distinct, observable effects.
7. Retired identities cannot be selected for new games or reassigned; profile changes and deletion do not alter supported canonical replay integrity. Whole-club deletion removes the club's identities.

## Verification

Exercise the permission matrix with managers, regular members, unauthenticated actors and members of a different club. Verify server responses and persisted effects, not only hidden UI controls. Cover consent changes, active hosting/participation and deletion using completed games shared with other players. Verify stable opaque identities and replay hashes, retained individual and other-player results, erased profile fields, localized anonymous display, and exclusion from new selection. Check both individual and whole-club deletion without assuming old named records have been anonymized.
