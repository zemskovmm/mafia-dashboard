# IDD-0003.spec-host-controls-and-presentation

## Intent

Give an in-person moderator clear, touch-friendly controls while keeping role information appropriately concealed and auxiliary controls independent from game rules.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines access and participant identity.
- IDD-0002 owns phase, voting and outcome semantics.
- IDD-0004 owns durable game state and correction.

## Behavior

### Dashboard and language

- Present the application as **La Verdad**, the official application of the club in Málaga, with a link to `https://mafiamalaga.es`. The entry screen and browser icon use the club's gold fedora figure and circular border on black, rendered as scalable vector artwork with transparent outer corners. Browser titles use La Verdad, qualified by the current club name without repeating an identical name. Official-club attribution follows the existing interface language. Application branding does not rename stored clubs or replace their customized header and playing-card logos.
- Show the current phase, ten ordered seats and the next legal host action clearly. Live controls must remain reachable at the supported landscape viewports: 1366 × 768 in Chromium, 1366 × 1024 in WebKit, and 1000 × 600 in Chromium.
- Support keyboard operation, visible focus, accessible control names, core touch targets of at least 48 × 48 CSS pixels, reduced motion and status indications that do not depend on color alone. Seat dragging is a mouse/touch-only exception; ordinary player pickers and buttons retain their keyboard operation.
- Support English and Russian. Before club entry, derive the language from the browser; within a club, use its selected language. Language changes do not change rules or canonical game state.
- Show the captured rules edition during player setup and clearly identify nonstandard club deviations. Do not repeat the dated rules-edition row in Role giveaway or above the live or preview phase/day heading. Restoring an older game must not present it as the new federation edition.
- Use consistent responsive spacing between the screen edges, main panels, visible title/toolbar row and toolbar icons. The main table and timer/music panels share the same outer border treatment and aligned bottom edges, with bottom gutters matching side gutters. At the large landscape layout, accommodate the bottom platform safe area inside the panels rather than enlarging their outer margin; controls remain above the protected area. Measure header clearance from the visible title and control bounds, not an oversized row with additional internal gaps.
- Table action buttons have outer rounded corners matching the table cards, including role-giveaway advancement. Adjacent seat actions form one joined group with straight internal edges; single controls remain rounded on all corners. Preserve touch dimensions, focus and enabled/disabled behavior.
- Table player cards are translucent by default, with a single continuous glass perimeter and soft caustic highlights that respond to interaction and card transitions. Keep text crisp and status cues discernible, without nested hard outlines. Provide a usable translucent fallback where enhanced refraction is unavailable and an opaque, nonanimated presentation when the corresponding accessibility preferences require it. No continuous decorative movement is required during idle gameplay.

### Developer glass tuning

- The existing guarded developer drawer includes a collapsible, localized **Glass** section. **Enhanced glass** is a device-local opt-in, off by default and independent of player/status lighting. Show requested versus active rendering and a readable fallback reason; refraction is unavailable while enhanced rendering is inactive.
- Refraction strength ranges from 0 to 24, initially 10. **Gold background light** ranges from 0 to 100%, initially 25% of the full broad decorative wash. These are tuning values, not calibrated physical units. Zero refraction retains glass edges and semantic cues; zero gold removes the broad wash without dimming edges, status glow, labels or focus. Gold tuning also works in fallback presentation.
- Settings change the visible game presentation live, persist only on this browser/device and survive hiding developer access. Reset restores the default-off toggle and these two defaults, without changing other appearance settings. Invalid values use safe defaults; blocked storage leaves session controls usable. Display changes never create game events or change votes.
- Enhanced optics sample the same visible decorative scene beneath player cards and nomination rows, not arbitrary text, logos or media. Keep sharp content and semantic touch/keyboard controls above decoration. Nomination glass is static rather than another animated sweep. Preserve theme, background shades, day/night distinction, role privacy, voted-card transitions and all voting semantics.
- Keep readable materials until enhanced rendering succeeds and during failure or context loss. Honor reduced transparency/motion, resize, scrolling and app return; hidden/idle optical rendering stops and leaving the game presentation releases its resources. Physical Home Screen qualification remains distinct from browser rendering evidence.

### Player setup and correction

- Integrate numbered card-style player pickers into ordinary Game setup, retaining the separate host picker, Casper option and existing setup actions. Do not add a separate seat-arrangement screen, entry button or selected-player side panel.
- Drag a dedicated seat handle with mouse or touch to swap occupants; clicking a player's name opens searchable reselection. Searching is provisional: only selecting a different identity or explicitly clearing the field changes the roster. Escape, blur and unchanged selection preserve the prior selection and deal.
- Keep normal touch scrolling outside handles. Show discernible drag-source and valid-target cues; commit only a valid final drop. Cancellation, release outside seats or a changed roster during the drag must not commit a stale target.
- On Role giveaway, place a standard-sized **Player setup** back button immediately before the title. It returns to the same setup and restores heading focus. No New badge, persistent reset-warning bar or edit-confirmation dialog is shown; actual edits follow IDD-0002's whole-deal reset rule.
- Keep remaining-role progress and completed assignment feedback in the table center, without a duplicate top progress banner.

### Role privacy

- When daytime masking is enabled, conceal role-revealing labels and highlights from active players who might see the display.
- Night and deliberate role giveaway are exempt from this masking requirement.
- This is a host-operated screen, not a public spectator display or a guarantee that every host secret is concealed in every phase.

### Music and timer

- Music is optional and must not block setup or game start when playback is unavailable.
- Support visible embedded YouTube playback, queue navigation and fades. Local queue edits must not mutate the owner's original YouTube playlist.
- **Play next** places the selected entry immediately after the current track without restarting, pausing or reloading current playback. Repeated edits preserve entry identity and the selected order through the next track transition; paused playback stays paused.
- Preserve YouTube's built-in Next and Previous navigation rather than replacing them with application buttons. Native Next follows the session queue when the embedded player has a following entry. When a successor is queued after the last entry already loaded by YouTube, its native Next button may remain unavailable until the track finishes; natural completion must still play the queued successor, and explicit **Play now** remains available.
- Show the YouTube Music playlist selector beside **Session queue** in the expanded player, including when no playlist is selected. Do not show it in Music player setup or the collapsed player. Keep account connection/disconnection and explicit player reload in setup, with accessible loading, retry and reconnection feedback. Expanding or collapsing the player alone does not interrupt playback.
- Playback and fades are auxiliary local controls, not durable game events.
- Timer presets of 10, 20, 30 and 60 seconds immediately replace the current countdown. Tapping the timer face pauses or resumes it. Recommended durations follow the applicable procedure: Sheriff greeting/checks 10, Best Move 20, tie/restored free-seating/short-table speech 30, normal speech/Mafia planning 60 seconds; starting the timer remains explicit.
- The timer warning has a visible equivalent when optional audio is unavailable. Timer expiry never advances the game automatically.

### Judge procedure and Best Move

- Opening-night guidance retains Mafia agreement and explicit Mafia-asleep confirmation, then separately reminds the host to wake Sheriff for ten seconds to look around the table and greet the host. Highlight the Sheriff card blue during this greeting. Offer **Begin round zero** directly afterward, with no masked-identification wording, Don-confirmation screen or separate free-seating stop in the new journey. Supported saved intermediate steps remain readable and explicitly continuable without automatically rewriting them.
- Ordinary-night controls retain all ten target seats, the check window and target-seat readback without the redundant instruction to call every seat, even inactive ones. Sheriff result guidance is red/thumb up and black/thumb down, preserving secret-state boundaries.
- Observed multiple shooting is an in-place recording mode, not a dialog or separate screen. Preserve recorded assignments and allow repeated shooter/target taps to append additional private physical-shot observations without a UI count limit. Entering the mode alone records no shot. Actual multiple shots always result in a miss, even at the same target; sanctions remain separate. Keep the observations available for later host review and do not silently erase them through ordinary transcription correction.
- Best Move stays in night presentation until acceptance. Show the ordered accepted numbers, a twenty-second timer recommendation, readback/acceptance guidance and explicit early-completion actions.
- Provide **Skip Best Move** when the accepted list is empty. Skip records a decline for this attempt and continues normally without changing preferences. With accepted numbers, **Finish early** retains the prefix. Obvious-slip correction is explicit and distinct from ordinary selection; accepted numbers cannot silently be toggled away.
- The judge records physical observations and confirms external reviews. UI timing and prompts do not certify speech, gestures, masks or video-review performance.

### Voting presentation

- During voting, each whole nomination row is an accessible touch/keyboard selection target, without a separate inner name button. Rows and their selection/focus outlines have square corners in both ordinary and enhanced-glass presentation; player cards around the table retain their rounded corners. The selected candidate is the recipient of player taps; ordinary transcription correction is direct and needs no explanation form. Next candidate and Finish voting are explicit actions, without announcement, mention or STOP confirmation controls.
- A technical dispute prominently suspends call progression and ballot completion until the judge acknowledges external video review and accepts the final count. Keep appropriate discipline controls available; do not disguise correction as a participant's second vote.
- Show the nomination list only when nonempty during daytime discussion, voting, tie procedures and daytime farewell until night begins. Hide it during setup, night (including night-origin farewell), terminal states and empty days without reserving empty panel space. Remove the disclosure reminder, redundant procedural copy and aggregate active-vote summary at every count; retain per-candidate vote totals, timer and music. No spectator-display confidentiality guarantee is added.
- Keep one Rules entry point, in its voting position and reachable during other applicable phases. It combines discipline actions first, relevant voting exceptions and optional rule reference; remove the duplicate Judge discipline entry point while preserving player shortcuts and actionable unresolved-state warnings.
- Removal-rule choices show their unchanged numbers with accurate localized descriptions of at most two words. Additional disqualification notes are labelled optional; a valid player, occurrence stage and removal rule suffice, including the known fourth-foul rule.
- Vote-count text in that panel is twice the reference text size of 0.72 times the root text size: 1.44 times the root text size. This applies to the panel's displayed vote counts without requiring unrelated controls to grow.
- **Finish voting** is the explicit normal completion action. Candidate navigation must not imply that the ballot has finished.
- Voters for the currently selected candidate share the undimmed active daytime speaker's visual emphasis, without being identified as the current speaker. Both active speakers and current-candidate voters grow to 120% of ordinary card width and height without moving neighboring seats. Coordinate the size change and light fade over approximately 250 ms on entry and exit, including speaker turns, candidate changes and cleared selection; rapid changes reverse smoothly. Voters assigned to other candidates return to their smaller, semi-transparent appearance; clearing a vote restores the ordinary unvoted appearance. These reversible size/opacity transitions are independent of role privacy and never delay concealment of private role cues. Preserve correction controls, focus, touch targets and card/action clearance; reduced motion makes transitions immediate. Separate Next candidate and Finish voting by a deliberate gap no smaller than the nearby card-to-action gap.
- A permitted eliminate-all car-crash count can be explicitly completed with no supporting votes, without selecting and deselecting players first. Preserve majority and eligibility rules; distinguish the host finishing the physical count from an unresolved provisional ballot.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Auxiliary timer and media actions must not mutate game rules or silently create durable game events. Language and display choices must preserve canonical state. Role-cue masking includes visual highlights, not just text labels, within its daytime boundary.

## Non-Goals

- Player-phone clients, public spectator layouts or mandatory working music before role drawing.
- A Stop-only timer, replacement-confirmation gate or required chime-test control.
- Locking the rendering framework, GPU effects or CSS implementation into product intent.
- Declaring a permanently mandatory media provider beyond the supported integration described here.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A moderator can reach and operate legal controls at supported landscape sizes, with discernible focus and non-color status cues, consistent screen/panel/header spacing and joined seat-action groups. Default translucent cards have one glass edge, crisp content and accessible fallbacks. Mouse/touch seat dragging is supported; other controls retain keyboard operation. Role giveaway has no edition/date row or duplicate top counter, while central assignment progress, player-setup edition identity and deviation disclosure remain available.
2. English/Russian switching preserves game state and follows the pre-club versus club language boundary. Entry, loading and sign-in states retain La Verdad identity and localized official-club attribution; browser titles and application messages use the same name. Branding assets load from the application itself without requiring the club website, and existing club names and customized logos remain unchanged.
3. Daytime masking conceals role labels and revealing highlights; it does not mask deliberate role giveaway or night information.
4. Unavailable music or optional audio does not prevent game setup or timer use. Local playlist edits leave the source playlist unchanged. Play next preserves current playback and plays the selected entry next, including after repeated edits. Built-in Next follows the session queue, subject only to the last-loaded-entry limitation above; natural completion and Play now still reach that queued successor. Native Previous retains correct entry identity. The playlist selector is reachable beside Session queue only in the expanded player, not in setup; account management and explicit reload remain in setup.
5. Preset replacement and Pause/Resume behave predictably; warning and expiry never advance phases.
6. Whole-row nominee selection and direct vote correction work by touch and keyboard; current-candidate voters and the daytime speaker grow to 120% with coordinated reversible size/light transitions, other candidates' voters shrink/fade, and cleared votes restore the ordinary appearance, with stationary neighboring seats and separated completion actions. Disputes stop progression until a final ruling. Empty/nighttime nomination panels are absent, and Rules combines discipline and exceptions without duplicate entry points. Removal options have short meaningful labels and permit blank additional notes. Panel vote-count typography meets the specified sizing at equivalent root text size and zoom. A permitted collective count can finish with zero supporting votes without altering majority or eligibility rules.
7. The streamlined opening offers a separate ten-second Sheriff greeting with a blue Sheriff-card cue after Mafia-asleep confirmation, then begins the zero round directly. Ordinary check guidance and supported legacy gameplay remain unchanged. Best Move offers explicit Skip for an empty list and prefix-preserving Finish early, remains night until accepted, and has an actual twenty-second timer preset without automatic phase progression.
8. Extra physical shots can be recorded repeatedly on the night table without opening a dialog, remain privately reviewable after restoration, and always cause a miss when a shooter fired more than once. Mode activation alone records nothing. The redundant all-seat instruction is absent while all ten target controls remain reachable.
9. Developer Glass controls are localized, guarded and operable by touch/keyboard. Opt-in rendering, independent live refraction/gold tuning, browser-local persistence, safe invalid/storage-denied behavior and isolated reset meet the defaults above. Enhanced/fallback presentation preserves game state, semantic player glow, focus, privacy and voting controls, with static nomination glass and readable accessibility/context-loss fallback.

## Verification

Compare current-candidate voters with the active daytime speaker, then advance turns and re-select candidates: active cards reach 120% while previous voters become smaller and subdued, without changing assignments or counts. Inspect intermediate size and light states in both directions, including rapid reversal and clearing; outgoing light fades rather than disappearing abruptly. Clear and reassign votes, including automatic final-candidate assignments, with immediate role masking and reduced-motion transitions. Inspect fixed seat positions and card/action clearance in ordinary and enhanced glass; collective ballots and Best Move retain their own presentation.

Compare both main-panel outlines and aligned bottoms against side gutters in normal and fullscreen landscape presentation, including safe-area insets. Inspect ordinary, selected and keyboard-focused nomination rows in fallback and enhanced glass: corners stay square, candidate totals remain correct and no aggregate active-vote line or reserved gap appears in either language. Table-player corners and whole-row selection remain unchanged.

Exercise developer Glass controls in both languages and lighting modes, including reload, reset, hidden developer access, malformed settings and denied storage. Compare zero/default/maximum refraction and gold over the real decorative scene; verify actual displaced pixels as well as screenshots. Check preserved glow/settings and game state, whole-row edge/name/count selection, voted-card correction, disputes, panel scrolling, rotation and app return at supported viewports. Verify reduced transparency/motion, graphics failure/recovery, bounded idle/hidden work and resource release. Record actual physical iPad Home Screen touch/rotation/return acceptance separately; desktop browser evidence does not qualify that device.

Inspect the black-and-gold vector emblem enlarged and at favicon sizes. Exercise entry, loading, unavailable sign-in, club entry and exit in both languages, checking browser titles, attribution, link destination and preserved club names/logos. Verify that branding does not obstruct existing controls at supported landscape sizes and remains available without requests to the club website.

Verify Play next during active and paused playback, repeated edits and duplicate tracks, natural completion, explicit navigation and playlist replacement. Click actual embedded Next and Previous controls before and after queue edits and verify the selected entries, not just eventual playback. Exercise queueing a successor from the last loaded entry: record native Next availability while requiring natural progression and Play now to work. Check uninterrupted current playback and fade/pause/resume across transitions in a real embedded session, not only the displayed queue order. Exercise playlist selection without opening setup, loading/error recovery, empty selection, account disconnect and expansion/collapse; verify dropdown placement and bounded controls with long playlist titles in both languages at supported sizes.

Verify integrated setup through real mouse and touch gestures, searchable replacement and cancellation, fixed Casper, and reachable seat/control targets at the supported viewports. Verify the back button precedes the role-giveaway title without obstructing progress or other controls. No keyboard-drag gesture is required.

Exercise real controls and representative layout states, including keyboard focus, accessible names, reduced motion and non-color cues. Compare daytime, night and role-giveaway privacy states rather than assuming hidden badges prove concealment. Verify media failures, source-playlist preservation, timer replacement/pause/expiry, localization and candidate navigation. Measure voting-count text at equal viewport, root text size and zoom; unrelated layout behavior must remain usable.

Exercise the shortened opening sequence, blue Sheriff greeting, check readback/gestures, dispute pause and recovery, and Best Move Skip/partial completion with real controls in both languages. Verify actual recommended timer values rather than button labels alone; accepted numbers and preferences survive early completion and restoration unchanged. Inspect Role giveaway/live/preview heading-label absence with retained central progress and deviation disclosure. Compare outer and joined inner corner radii and screenshots across supported layouts, and measure actual title/control bounds against panel and screen edges. Inspect default glass and highlights over real backgrounds, animated voted/unvoted transitions, reduced motion/transparency and refraction fallback. Exercise explicit zero-support collective completion and repeated same/different-target physical shots, including mode re-entry, private review, replay and restoration; preserve ordinary correction and supported history integrity.
