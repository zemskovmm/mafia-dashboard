# IDD-0003.spec-host-controls-and-presentation

## Intent

Give an in-person moderator clear, touch-friendly controls while keeping role information appropriately concealed and auxiliary controls independent from game rules.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines access and participant identity.
- IDD-0002 owns phase, voting and outcome semantics.
- IDD-0004 owns durable game state and correction.

## Behavior

### Dashboard and language

- Present the application as **La Verdad**. The entry screen and browser icon retain the gold fedora figure and circular border on black, rendered as scalable vector artwork with transparent outer corners. Browser titles use La Verdad, qualified by the current club name without repeating an identical name. The ordinary club-entry heading is **The city sleeps. The game begins.** in English and **Город засыпает. Игра начинается.** in Russian, without the former official-club attribution/link or Choose your club heading. Club creation and editing retain their descriptive titles. Application branding does not rename stored clubs or replace their customized header and playing-card logos.
- On the signed-in club list, place the account avatar, name, email and Sign out in the upper-right corner. Keep them absent from signed-out states and club creation/editing, and preserve pending-action disabling. The club portal has no glass-colour picker; the in-club Manage setting and saved theme remain available and continue to colour the portal. Account controls and long localized headings remain reachable and non-overlapping on narrow screens as well as supported landscape layouts.
- Show the current phase, ten ordered seats and the next legal host action clearly. Live controls must remain reachable at the supported landscape viewports: 1366 × 768 in Chromium, 1366 × 1024 in WebKit, and 1000 × 600 in Chromium.
- Support keyboard operation, visible focus, accessible control names, core touch targets of at least 48 × 48 CSS pixels, reduced motion and status indications that do not depend on color alone. Seat dragging is a mouse/touch-only exception; ordinary player pickers and buttons retain their keyboard operation.
- Hide the application fullscreen control when fullscreen is unavailable or the application is already launched in Home Screen/standalone display mode. Supported browser fullscreen retains working Enter and Exit controls without an empty toolbar slot for an unavailable action.
- Support English and Russian. Before club entry, derive the language from the browser; within a club, use its selected language. Language changes do not change rules or canonical game state.
- Show the captured rules edition during player setup. Do not show nonstandard/club-deviation warnings, acknowledgement controls or physical-deal admonitions anywhere in setup, live play or preview. Do not repeat the dated rules-edition row in Role giveaway or above the live or preview phase/day heading. Restoring an older game must not present it as the new federation edition.
- Use consistent responsive spacing between the screen edges, main panels, visible title/toolbar row and toolbar icons. The main table and timer/music panels share the same outer border treatment and aligned bottom edges, with bottom gutters matching side gutters. At the large landscape layout, accommodate the bottom platform safe area inside the panels rather than enlarging their outer margin; controls remain above the protected area. Measure header clearance from the visible title and control bounds, not an oversized row with additional internal gaps.
- Table action buttons have outer rounded corners matching the table cards, including role-giveaway advancement. Adjacent seat actions form one joined group with straight internal edges; single controls remain rounded on all corners. Preserve touch dimensions, focus and enabled/disabled behavior.
- Player-card surfaces share one translucent glass character across the game table and its setup/live/preview uses, Available players, setup seat pickers, player profiles, deceased-player identity and nomination rows. Preserve their existing dimensions, content and controls; Available players adopts the table's rounded corners while other families retain their geometry. Use a single continuous glass perimeter and soft caustic highlights, with crisp text and discernible status cues rather than nested hard outlines. Provide a readable fallback when enhanced refraction is unavailable and an opaque, nonanimated presentation when accessibility preferences require it. No continuous decorative movement is required at idle; plain names, badges and nested action buttons do not become additional cards.
- Available players uses the main table's player-card material, rounded corners, neutral styling and warm gold selected perimeter instead of a separate card skin or fire. Shared glass controls apply to both screens without availability-only tuning. Its binary selection remains immediately visible independently of graphics readiness, with existing layout, accessible selection and actions preserved. The static default Noir light background remains availability-only; gameplay fire, meaningful role/turn lighting and day/night presentation remain unchanged.

### Club-entry presentation and Fancy stuff

- The existing guarded developer drawer includes a collapsed, localized **Fancy stuff** section with an individual **Club entry effects** switch, on by default. Remember the choice only in this browser/device, preserving an explicit saved OFF value. Missing or malformed settings use the default; unavailable storage leaves the current session's control usable. Other visual settings and gameplay/server records are unchanged.
- When enabled, existing club cards retain their layout, text, customized logos and transformed rounded outlines. Their edges quietly smoulder and intensify on hover or keyboard focus; New club retains ordinary creation behavior. Accepted activation ignites the entire outline immediately: the real face burns inward in approximately 360 ms while the real picker burns outward simultaneously over approximately 1800 ms to reveal the actual workspace. Preserve branding, background, saved colour and scroll position.
- When disabled, show no club-entry embers, static ember decoration or burn transition. Enter the actual workspace without an artificial animation delay, while still waiting for legitimate access and browser-storage readiness. Returning to the picker reflects the current setting; re-enabling restores the same accepted effect.
- Keep the outgoing language stable while the workspace adopts its club language. Prevent duplicate activation and click-through, then restore interaction and focus when the real workspace is available, including after a bounded visual wait ends first. Decoration never grants access, bypasses ownership or recovery, discards pending work or creates game state. Automatic restoration/recovery without card activation does not replay the entrance; refused entry leaves recovery controls usable.
- Reduced motion avoids continuous embers and sweeping burn even when the setting is on. Graphics failure, context loss, delayed readiness, resize, hidden-page state and unmount must not trap navigation or leave blocking decoration; hidden, offscreen and abandoned effects do not sustain unnecessary rendering.

### Compact player-card actions and status

- Replace the persistent player-card foul button with a clearly downward swipe or mouse drag. It issues one eligible foul, never an accompanying tap action. Keep upward penalty and horizontal context gestures distinct. Cancelled, multi-touch, short or ambiguous gestures do nothing; ordinary scrolling outside cards remains available. A focused card also accepts the localized, discoverable F shortcut without intercepting typing or repeated keypresses elsewhere.
- Place up to three red foul dots to the right of the person, with a crossed-out speech-bubble marker for pending mute. Preserve actual foul counts and accessible descriptions, existing restriction consumption and the fourth-foul discipline flow; the dots are not a cap on the domain count.
- During discussion, tapping the whole eligible card nominates, supports or corrects the current speaker's own selection under IDD-0002. Remove the redundant card nomination button. A rejected second nomination must not disable supports, fouls or context actions. Other phases retain their actual voting, night and setup actions.
- Distinguish round-wide nomination/support from the current speaker's selection: use left-side nomination and additional-supporter markers, with a separate mint check and optional subtle outline for this speaker. Additional-supporter counts exclude the original nominator. Use consistent visual slots, compatible glyph sizing and aligned counts without stretching icons or changing card/person/name dimensions or table positions. Status remains understandable without tint/color alone and in read-only history.

### Developer glass tuning

- The existing guarded developer drawer includes a collapsible, localized **Glass** section with essentials and grouped fine tuning. **Enhanced glass** is device-local, on for new/reset settings and independent of player/status lighting. Preserve an existing valid saved OFF preference. Show requested versus active rendering and a readable fallback reason; refraction controls are unavailable while enhanced rendering is inactive. Default rendering does not require opening developer access.
- Refraction strength ranges from 0 to 24, initially 14. The default **Reference-inspired edge** profile bends the decorative scene near card edges while preserving centres; **Current simple** remains selectable globally. **Gold background light** ranges from 0 to 100%, initially 25% of the broad decorative wash. These are tuning values, not calibrated physical units. Zero refraction retains glass edges and semantic cues; zero gold removes the broad wash without dimming edges, status glow, labels or focus. Gold tuning also works in fallback presentation.
- **Matte glass**, on by default, independently switches all player-card materials between frosted and clearer finishes. **Frosting amount** ranges from 0 to 100%, initially 50%, retaining its saved amount when Matte is off. Matte/frosting do not change refraction, tint, light or semantic cues. **Glass tint** ranges from 0 to 30%, initially 13%, adjusting only the decorative tint rather than content opacity. Reduced transparency overrides clear/tint choices with readable opaque material.
- **Edge highlights** and **Caustic light** independently range from 0 to 100%, initially 100%; their zero values remove only their decorative contribution, never a required host boundary, focus, selected state or gameplay light. Material tuning works in enhanced and fallback presentation where applicable, with truthful disabled reasons. There is no separate Available players glass or Noir contrast control. Controls show values/units, remain keyboard/touch operable in compact layouts and do not duplicate gameplay-fire settings.
- Settings update every shared player-card material live, persist only on this browser/device and survive hiding developer access. Reset restores the Glass defaults above without changing other appearance preferences. Missing or invalid fields default independently; ignore the retired Noir contrast field while preserving valid old enabled/refraction/gold/matte and other retained choices, and keep session controls usable when storage is denied. Display changes never create game events, change votes or synchronize through server/game records.
- Enhanced optics sample the same visible decorative scene beneath each card, not arbitrary text, logos or media. Keep sharp content and semantic controls above decoration, with no extra lens on nested controls. Nomination glass stays static and square. Preserve existing non-availability backgrounds, theme/shades, day/night distinction, role privacy, voted-card transitions, form/popover/drag behavior and voting semantics.
- Keep readable materials on every surface until its enhanced rendering succeeds and during failure or context loss. Honor reduced transparency/motion, clipping, resize, scrolling and app return; hidden/idle optical rendering stops, offscreen lists do not sustain needless work, and leaving eligible presentation releases resources. Physical Home Screen qualification remains distinct from browser evidence.
- The Glass section also tunes player-card nomination tint (0–50%, initially 18%) and color (initially warm gold #e6bc7e), Icons/Tint/Combined treatment (initially Combined), ballot/flag nomination glyph (initially ballot), raised-hand/thumbs-up support glyph (initially raised hand), and check with/without subtle outline (initially with outline). Keep the mute speech bubble fixed. These device-local controls update real card materials and glyphs live, share existing persistence/reset safeguards and never mutate game data. Essential counts and selection/mute indicators remain discernible at zero tint, in tint-only treatment and with enhanced glass unavailable; other semantic highlights retain their meaning.

### Player exit animation

- A player leaving normal play lifts slightly and turns sideways through 180 degrees while dropping to the existing eliminated-card dimensions. The revealed face already bears the deceased cross; identity, seat number and any permitted role text remain upright and unmirrored. Preserve neighboring seat positions, ordinary glass materials and action eligibility; add no bounce.
- Ordinary departures animate only after that player's goodbye is completed. Where existing rules omit goodbye, animate at the public daytime or terminal departure boundary, never on a private night calculation or provisional shot marker. Accepted disciplinary exclusion animates immediately, including a fourth foul or exclusion during goodbye, without waiting for its later announcement or changing physical-departure/check obligations. Each actual departure animates once, including multiple eligible departures in one transition.
- The existing guarded settings drawer includes a localized **Player exit animation** section. Default it on with a **100 ms lift**, **480 ms landing**, **100% lift/flip overlap** and **Ease out** at **100% strength** for lift, flip and drop. Ease out uses `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.58, 1)`. Lift and rotation begin together; drop starts at the lift apex, with rotation and drop ending together after 580 ms. The slight lift is 10 CSS pixels with 7% transient enlargement, returning to existing card dimensions.
- Provide enabled/disabled, lift duration (80–700 ms), landing duration (200–1400 ms), overlap (0–100%), independently selected easing/strength (0–100%), reset to these defaults and a harmless sample preview. Zero overlap waits for pickup to complete; full overlap starts both movements together. Landing duration always measures apex to final stop. Show resolved timing/units; settings persist locally on this browser, not in game or server records, and remain usable when storage is invalid or denied.
- Motion preferences apply to the next departure, not by restarting an in-progress departure. Disabled or reduced motion shows the correct static state immediately. Initial loading, restored games, history previews, Undo, Reset and session replacement do not replay old departures; obsolete or interrupted motion is cleaned up. Canonical transitions, saves, counts and legal actions are never delayed by animation. Live role masking also applies to animated faces, including when presentation crosses into another phase.

### Player setup and correction

- Integrate numbered card-style player pickers into ordinary Game setup, retaining the separate host picker, Casper option and existing setup actions. Do not add a separate seat-arrangement screen, entry button or selected-player side panel.
- Drag a dedicated seat handle with mouse or touch to swap occupants; clicking a player's name opens searchable reselection. Searching is provisional: only selecting a different identity or explicitly clearing the field changes the roster. Escape, blur and unchanged selection preserve the prior selection and deal.
- Keep normal touch scrolling outside handles. Show discernible drag-source and valid-target cues; commit only a valid final drop. Cancellation, release outside seats or a changed roster during the drag must not commit a stale target.
- On Role giveaway, place a standard-sized **Player setup** back button immediately before the title. It returns to the same setup and restores heading focus. No New badge, persistent reset-warning bar or edit-confirmation dialog is shown; actual edits follow IDD-0002's whole-deal reset rule.
- Keep remaining-role progress and completed assignment feedback in the table center, without a duplicate top progress banner.
- Three taps on an assigned human role card reroll immediately when cards remain available. Either horizontal swipe opens a simple selected-seat menu to clear the role or choose an available role, including after the last card is dealt. Opening or dismissing it never draws/rerolls or retains partial reroll taps; vertical and cancelled gestures do not open it. Keep keyboard context-menu access, Escape dismissal and focus restoration. Pending save/creation safeguards and Casper's fixed role remain enforced.

### Role privacy

- When daytime masking is enabled, conceal role-revealing labels and highlights from active players who might see the display.
- Night and deliberate role giveaway are exempt from this masking requirement.
- This is a host-operated screen, not a public spectator display or a guarantee that every host secret is concealed in every phase.

### Music and timer

- Music is optional and must not block setup or game start when playback is unavailable.
- Support visible embedded YouTube playback, queue navigation and fades. Local queue edits must not mutate the owner's original YouTube playlist.
- **Play next** places the selected entry immediately after the current track without restarting, pausing or reloading current playback. Repeated edits preserve entry identity and the selected order through the next track transition; paused playback stays paused.
- Preserve YouTube's built-in Next and Previous navigation rather than replacing them with application buttons. Native Next follows the session queue when the embedded player has a following entry. When a successor is queued after the last entry already loaded by YouTube, its native Next button may remain unavailable until the track finishes; natural completion must still play the queued successor, and explicit **Play now** remains available.
- Show the YouTube Music playlist selector beside **Session queue** in the expanded player, including when no playlist is selected. Do not show it in Music player setup or the collapsed player. Keep account connection/disconnection and explicit player reload in setup, with accessible loading, retry and reconnection feedback. Expanding or collapsing the player alone does not interrupt playback.
- Playback and fades are auxiliary local controls, not durable game events.
- Keep the Fade in, Quick fade and Slow fade row together at the auxiliary card’s bottom, with no separate strip between the footer and the card’s inner border. Preserve normal inner button padding; add safe-area clearance inside the footer only when the existing outer gutter, border and normal padding do not already protect the controls. Expanding the player, hiding timer utilities or growing the nomination list must not displace or clip the fade row; overflowing utilities remain reachable by scrolling without changing the card’s dimensions.
- The Session queue playlist selector offers only titles beginning with **Mafia**, ignoring case and surrounding whitespace. Preserve original labels, ordering and identities. Explain when none match; filtering alone must not clear a restored playlist, change the source library or interrupt playback.
- Timer presets of 10, 20, 30 and 60 seconds immediately replace the current countdown. Tapping the timer face pauses or resumes it. Recommended durations follow the applicable procedure: Sheriff greeting/checks 10, Best Move 20, tie/restored free-seating/short-table speech 30, normal speech/Mafia planning 60 seconds; starting the timer remains explicit.
- Double-tapping the timer face resets its existing seconds display to zero and leaves it stopped, cancelling pending warning audio. Single taps retain pause/resume; a fresh preset is required to start after reset. Keyboard activation and ordinary scrolling are not double-tap gestures.
- The timer warning has a visible equivalent when optional audio is unavailable. Timer expiry never advances the game automatically.

### Judge procedure and Best Move

- Opening-night guidance retains Mafia agreement and explicit Mafia-asleep confirmation, then separately reminds the host to wake Sheriff for ten seconds to look around the table and greet the host. Highlight the Sheriff card blue during this greeting. Offer **Begin round zero** directly afterward, with no masked-identification wording, Don-confirmation screen or separate free-seating stop in the new journey. Supported saved intermediate steps remain readable and explicitly continuable without automatically rewriting them.
- Ordinary-night controls retain all ten target seats, the check window and target-seat readback without the redundant instruction to call every seat, even inactive ones. Sheriff result guidance is red/thumb up and black/thumb down, preserving secret-state boundaries.
- Observed multiple shooting is an in-place recording mode, not a dialog or separate screen. Preserve recorded assignments and allow repeated shooter/target taps to append additional private physical-shot observations without a UI count limit. Entering the mode alone records no shot. Actual multiple shots always result in a miss, even at the same target; sanctions remain separate. Keep the observations available for later host review and do not silently erase them through ordinary transcription correction.
- Keep original shots visibly selected while recording additional shots. The recording-mode button glows orange while active, with no explanatory, count or status text beneath it. Additional shooter/target pairs have gold outlines, including repeated shots at the original target without replacing its selected fill. Recorded-shot outlines survive mode exit and restoration; non-color accessible cues distinguish additional observations from editable primary assignments. Private history retains the original assignments, ordered additional shots and resolved outcome.
- Best Move stays in night presentation until acceptance. Show the ordered accepted numbers, a twenty-second timer recommendation, readback/acceptance guidance and explicit early-completion actions.
- Provide **Skip Best Move** when the accepted list is empty. Skip records a decline for this attempt and continues normally without changing preferences. With accepted numbers, **Finish early** retains the prefix. Obvious-slip correction is explicit and distinct from ordinary selection; accepted numbers cannot silently be toggled away.
- The judge records physical observations and confirms external reviews. UI timing and prompts do not certify speech, gestures, masks or video-review performance.

### Voting presentation

- During voting, each whole nomination row is an accessible touch/keyboard selection target, without a separate inner name button. Rows and their selection/focus outlines have square corners in both ordinary and enhanced-glass presentation; player cards around the table retain their rounded corners. The selected candidate is the recipient of player taps; ordinary transcription correction is direct and needs no explanation form. Next candidate and Finish voting are explicit actions, without announcement, mention or STOP confirmation controls.
- A technical dispute prominently suspends call progression and ballot completion until the judge acknowledges external video review and accepts the final count. Keep appropriate discipline controls available; do not disguise correction as a participant's second vote.
- Show the nomination list only when nonempty during daytime discussion, voting, tie procedures and daytime farewell until night begins. Hide it during setup, night (including night-origin farewell), terminal states and empty days without reserving empty panel space. Remove the disclosure reminder, redundant procedural copy and aggregate active-vote summary at every count; retain per-candidate vote totals, timer and music. No spectator-display confidentiality guarantee is added.
- Keep one Rules entry point as a book-icon button in the top application toolbar, with an accessible localized name. It opens the read-only illustrated rules book directly, not the penalty menu. Do not retain a second table-positioned text entry or the old Rule reference section.
- Swiping up on a player card opens the penalty menu for that player throughout active play after role giveaway, including round zero, voting, night and result/farewell stages. Opening or dismissing it applies no penalty and must not also activate the card. Preserve horizontal player gestures and the separate music-panel swipe. Preview, role giveaway and terminal outcomes do not accept new penalties. During voting, this menu also retains clearly labelled ballot-wide dispute and review tools with unaccepted review inputs preserved across ordinary dismissal; actionable unresolved-state warnings remain available.
- The player shortcut names its fixed subject, defaults to disqualification and also offers opposing-team victory under IDD-0002's existing reason and committee-agreement requirements. Ordinary fouls use the separate downward card gesture and keyboard alternative, without persistent foul buttons. Prefill and show current-stage timing; non-shortcut disciplinary forms retain explicit timing choices. Shooting defaults to the count boundary, shot results to the completed-result boundary, and farewell to farewell timing. Do not infer that a role owner's physical check is already underway; preserve explicit confirmation of that fact. Changed game or phase context invalidates an open request.
- Make the penalty popup wider within the viewport so rule choices remain readable in both languages. To the right of each removal or opposing-victory rule selector, provide a non-submitting book button that opens the exact selected numbered rule. Empty selections cannot open an unrelated page; the implicit fourth-foul selection opens 6.2.1 even when its selector is disabled. Closing the book restores the unchanged unfinished form and its invoking control, including subject, action, timing, rule, notes, check-in-progress, committee agreement and validation. Reading never applies a decision; only the foreground reader handles focus and dismissal while its lower dialog is blurred and noninteractive. Preserve optional removal notes, role privacy, existing legality guards and stale-context invalidation.
- Removal-rule choices show their unchanged numbers with accurate localized descriptions of at most two words. Additional disqualification notes are labelled optional; a valid player, occurrence stage and removal rule suffice, including the known fourth-foul rule.
- Vote-count text in that panel is twice the reference text size of 0.72 times the root text size: 1.44 times the root text size. This applies to the panel's displayed vote counts without requiring unrelated controls to grow.
- **Finish voting** is the explicit normal completion action. Candidate navigation must not imply that the ballot has finished.
- Voters for the currently selected candidate share the undimmed active daytime speaker's visual emphasis, without being identified as the current speaker. Both active speakers and current-candidate voters grow to 120% of ordinary card width and height without moving neighboring seats. Coordinate the size change and light fade over approximately 250 ms on entry and exit, including speaker turns, candidate changes and cleared selection; rapid changes reverse smoothly. Voters assigned to other candidates return to their smaller, semi-transparent appearance; clearing a vote restores the ordinary unvoted appearance. These reversible size/opacity transitions are independent of role privacy and never delay concealment of private role cues. Preserve correction controls, focus, touch targets and card/action clearance; reduced motion makes transitions immediate. Separate Next candidate and Finish voting by a deliberate gap no smaller than the nearby card-to-action gap.
- A permitted eliminate-all car-crash count can be explicitly completed with no supporting votes, without selecting and deselecting players first. Preserve majority and eligibility rules; distinguish the host finishing the physical count from an unresolved provisional ballot.

### Illustrated rules book

- Present an inset floating paper book over the blurred real playing table, retaining ivory pages, serif typography, original illustrations, layered binding and tactile page curls. The book remains sharp; gameplay, timer and music remain mounted and otherwise unchanged. There is no surrounding desk, zoom/loupe control or bottom toolbar.
- A burgundy ribbon opens an actual contents page. Choosing a chapter visibly turns toward it with bounded animation. Clicking or dragging anywhere on paper, including text, art and margins, turns pages; embedded controls remain independently operable. Preserve vertical reading/scrolling, browser zoom, keyboard navigation, accessible page controls and immediate reduced-motion navigation.
- Adapt spread or single-page layout to available space with safe screen margins and readable, reachable content. Long references may occupy additional pages rather than being clipped or shrunk illegibly. Opening a selected reference immediately exposes its number and text, not merely an illustration. Every current removal and opposing-victory rule has a unique stable target in both languages; unsupported targets cannot silently show a different rule.
- Follow the application language without a separate persisted book preference. Clearly identify this as an authored club guide based on the dated MAFGAME source, not a complete or official translated tournament publication; distinguish app-specific behavior and retained legacy editions. Content is generic and never contains private live-game information. Bundle artwork and licensed fonts locally without third-party runtime dependencies.
- Closing by Escape, close action or outside the paper restores invoking focus and removes blur. Preserve position across ordinary reopening within the same game workspace, but cancel obsolete requests and motion on close, resize, game replacement or unmount. Reading never creates game events, saves or changes to recorded editions, privacy, selections or playback. Loading failure remains dismissible without losing the game or a disciplinary draft.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Auxiliary timer and media actions must not mutate game rules or silently create durable game events. Language and display choices must preserve canonical state. Role-cue masking includes visual highlights, not just text labels, within its daytime boundary.

## Non-Goals

- Player-phone clients, public spectator layouts or mandatory working music before role drawing.
- A Stop-only timer, replacement-confirmation gate or required chime-test control.
- Locking the rendering framework, GPU effects or CSS implementation into product intent.
- Declaring a permanently mandatory media provider beyond the supported integration described here.
