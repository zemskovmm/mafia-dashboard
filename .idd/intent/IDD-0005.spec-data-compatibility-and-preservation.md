# IDD-0005.spec-data-compatibility-and-preservation

## Intent

Preserve every game and known fact in one current data contract, with safe admission and an explicit one-time conversion boundary rather than ongoing older-engine compatibility.

## Related Specifications

- IDD-0001 defines consent and identity erasure with retained shared history.
- IDD-0002 defines the one current gameplay contract.
- IDD-0004 defines current game persistence, local recovery and deletion.
- IDD-0007 defines optional tournament metadata and derived statistics/rating.

## Behavior

### Storage compatibility

- Support explicitly initialized empty Azure storage or the recognized current store.
- Reject unrecognized, incompatible or incomplete stored layouts before modifying them. PostgreSQL schemas and SQL-to-Azure conversion are outside the supported storage boundary.
- Incompatibility must not trigger reset, broad backfill or deletion. Preserve the original data and report the unsupported state.

### Browser-local compatibility

- Previously supported browser/server records without optional tournament metadata remain supported as having no confirmed association. Pending association metadata is validated and preserved separately from canonical gameplay; absence is not permission to erase an association already saved on the server.

- Invalid or unsupported current browser-session data blocks gameplay until explicit clearance of the exact inspected record.
- Do not silently repair, migrate, overwrite or recover through an undisclosed fallback.
- Clearance must not erase a newer or different local record or unrelated local work merely because an earlier inspected record was invalid.

### Supported game meaning

- Support the current identity-only game format and its canonical replay integrity contract. Reject older name-bearing game snapshots, baselines, events, host material and results on admission rather than converting, stripping, rehashing or silently clearing them.
- Persisted business records have no rules-engine label or selector, including `rulesEdition`, `rulesetId` and `rulesetVersion`. There is one current gameplay/replay contract, no old/current game-bank separation, edition rejection policy or alternative runtime rules. Data-shape/operational versions remain permissible only without gameplay dispatch.
- Incomplete completed facts are valid under IDD-0004 and IDD-0007, not a compatibility error. A usable timeline is optional and independently validated. Invalid retained canonical material cannot reach editable play through injected defaults, old-engine reconstruction, rehashing or a displayed-snapshot fallback. A valid provisional draft remains distinguishable from unsupported canonical data.
- Preserve rejected originals without modification. Unsupported server records do not expose their identifying contents through supported game list, detail or event responses; browser-local invalid records block play until guarded clearance of the exact inspected record. Rejection is not an erasure of old stored names and does not authorize resetting a real database.
- Ordinary startup, reads, saves, retries and recovery do not perform historical decoding/conversion. Stored originals and unrelated pending work remain preserved when admission fails.
- Application rollback does not prove storage rollback or archive compatibility. Compatibility failure must preserve the original data rather than reset it to make an application start.

### Explicit one-time normalization

- Historical conversion is a deliberately invoked, bounded operation outside normal runtime, using private local operational material. Any predecessor decoder exists only there; it is not shipped as supported application compatibility, an importer or a recurring migration service. Inventory determines whether conversion is necessary; a conforming dataset has a verified no-op disposition.
- Preserve every game identity, club ownership, participant identity, accepted result, known observation and valid relationship, including tournaments, creation mappings and deletion markers. Do not join identities by display name, rerun historical gameplay to invent winners or infer absent roles, dates, fouls, Casper/roster facts or outcomes. Remove obsolete engine selectors, not arbitrary meaningful fields with historical-looking names.
- A separately authorized deletion of an exactly identified game may precede normalization under IDD-0004. Bind that decision to the inspected game and club, retain its recoverable original, and preserve deletion and creation-retry protections. This exception does not authorize the converter to discard failed records or extend deletion to another incompatible game; preserve every remaining game and known fact.
- Preserve completed timelines that can be converted faithfully under current parent/sequence/lifecycle/hash and secrecy requirements. Explicit representation changes may recompute corresponding hashes only with verified factual equivalence and a private disposition record; never use rehashing to conceal corruption. If completed replay detail cannot be retained faithfully, keep the game and all known facts as statistics-only. An active game or unsent draft that cannot be preserved truthfully is a stop condition, not clearance authority.
- Production conversion requires separately authorized target-bound write access, read-only inventory and exact dry-run dispositions, a complete private checksummed Table/Blob backup with isolated restore/readback proof, and an explicitly authorized compatible application release/maintenance sequence. Local approval, read access and an Azurite rehearsal do not satisfy those gates or authorize access grants, publication or deployment.
- Quiesce incompatible writers and protect pending-client work before cutover; use the existing owned freeze and atomic publication discipline. Bind changes to exact source identities/revisions/digests, stop on changed or unknown meaningful inputs, retain recovery originals, and prove interrupted/repeated execution is safe with a repeat no-op. Restore service only with compatible code and data. Restoring pre-conversion data after new writes is not a lossless rollback and requires a new recovery decision.

## Product-Significant Architecture And Constraints

Compatibility checks precede changes to unsupported data. Safe rejection is valid; silent conversion or deletion is not. The explicit one-time normalization exception does not weaken steady-state preservation, identity/privacy checks, atomic authority, idempotency, non-resurrection or the preservation of active/unsent work. It does not establish a permanent compatibility framework.

## Non-Goals

- General historical upgrades, automatic repair or indefinite compatibility with every past stored format.
- Reintroducing host claims or upgrading their retired database structure.
- Establishing numerical recovery objectives, backup-retention promises, hosting topology commitments or architecture durability.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Empty and recognized current Azure stores are supported; unrecognized, incompatible and incomplete stores are rejected without mutation or reset.
2. Unsupported browser-session data prevents play until explicit guarded clearance.
3. A newer or replaced local record survives an attempted clearance based on stale inspection.
4. Current games round-trip without engine metadata or old-rule dispatch. Minimal/partial completed facts remain usable independently of optional timeline detail, without old-game categories or unsupported-edition screens.
5. An incompatible rollback or recovery target remains preserved and is not automatically reset or backfilled.
6. Old name-bearing game payloads are refused without mutation or exposure as supported game content; supported identity-only records round-trip without copied profile fields and without server-side gameplay validation.
7. Maintained Azure backup/recovery preserves tournament identities and links when present and supported tournament-free content when absent. Recovery does not rewrite canonical material, and an older application is not assumed compatible with a restored store.
8. One-time normalization preserves every remaining game and known fact/relationship, retains faithful usable history, and records any completed statistics-only disposition. Any preceding deletion requires separate exact-record authorization, recoverable originals and non-resurrection protections. Another incompatible active/unsent state, corrupt authority, changed source or unknown meaningful data stops conversion without data loss.
9. Conversion is target-bound, recoverable, interruption-safe and a no-op on repetition. Production changes cannot proceed without the separate write, backup/recovery and compatible-release gates; no historical compatibility remains in normal runtime.

## Verification

Round-trip tournament-bearing and supported tournament-free material through maintained Azure backup/application readback, preserving canonical bytes, opaque references and creation/deletion semantics.

Compare stored data and structure before and after rejected compatibility operations, not only error messages. Exercise empty/current Azure stores and unrecognized or incomplete shapes. Reject name-bearing browser/server payloads and prove original bytes remain intact without their exposure through supported record views. Verify exact-record clearance against concurrently replaced local data, current identity-only live/replay round-trips, and partial completed records with absent history. Keep compatibility proof separate from any claim about production backup, name erasure in rejected originals or recovery readiness.

Rehearse one-time conversion against populated isolated Azure fixtures: complete/partial facts, faithful/absent/unconvertible completed timelines, active setup/play and unsent drafts, arbitrary meaningful optional observations, duplicate retries, changed source, interruption and a repeat no-op. Compare identities, relationships and known facts, not counts alone; prove restoration of original typed rows and referenced blobs and real application/browser readback of converted records. Verify ordinary runtime never decodes old rules or opportunistically converts records. Live completion requires exact-target manifests and actual authorized readbacks, not a local rehearsal or deployment response.

Exercise a separately authorized exact-game deletion before conversion: retain its recoverable original and non-resurrection protections, reject a changed deletion target, and prove all other games survive. An additional incompatible record must stop the remaining conversion rather than inherit the deletion exception.
